## LLM agents on interactive benchmarks [line 1]

**Long-Horizon-Terminal-Bench** ([arXiv:2607.08964](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08964), Jul 9, rev Jul 13)
— 46 long-horizon terminal tasks (incl. interactive games) with **dense reward-based grading**: fine-grained
graded subtasks give partial credit instead of binary pass/fail. Best model only 15.2% pass@1 at
partial-reward ≥0.95 (10.9% at 1.0); ~231 episodes / 9.9M tokens per task. **Verdict: PARK (context).**
External validation of exactly our completion-weighted-scorer reading: when grading is depth-weighted with
partial credit, the binding constraint is **long-horizon planning + iterative debugging depth**, not action
economy — the same "depth ≫ efficiency" conclusion that paused the sentinel efficiency line. No artifact to
lift (terminal domain, no ARC); usable as a one-line external citation if the depth-budget lane rationale
needs one.

## Test-time learning / banking-replay [lines 2+6]

**SWE-Replay: Efficient Test-Time Scaling for SWE Agents**
([arXiv:2601.22129](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.22129), Jan — out of window, new to set via citation trail)
— maintains an **archive of prior trajectories** and stochastically chooses explore-from-scratch vs.
**exploit-by-branching at critical intermediate steps**; −17.4% compute, +3.8% resolve on SWE-Bench
Verified; explicitly avoids value-estimate-based selection as unreliable. **Verdict: PARK, banking line.**
This is the cleanest external statement of our banked-prefix replay idea (branch from a banked mid-point
instead of resampling whole episodes). Two reasons it stays parked: (i) branching-from-intermediate-state
presumes faithful state restoration — in ARC-AGI-3 that means action-prefix replay into a latent state, and
the **state-aliasing root cause / latent-state audit still blocks exactly this** (a banked prefix that
aliases to a different latent state poisons the branch); (ii) headline gain is +3.8% — modest even where
state restoration is exact. Re-open only if the latent-state audit discharges.

**BAGEN: Are LLM Agents Budget-Aware?** ([arXiv:2606.00198](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.00198), May 29) —
agents can't estimate remaining budget (interval coverage 47% even post-training); early-stop saves 28–64%
tokens on failing trajectories **but only after SFT+RL**. **Verdict: IGNORE.** Requires weight updates (out
of regime) and serves the efficiency objective we've deliberately deprioritized — under the
completion-weighted scorer, early-stopping is the wrong lever vs. depth.

## Harness design / evolution [line 4]

**Delivery, Not Storage: Cue-Anchored Working Memory as a Harness Property**
([arXiv:2607.20972](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.20972), **Jul 23 — in window**) — empirical: agents given a
voluntary memory store performed **0 memory operations across 114 turns**; facts survive compaction only
when the **harness deterministically injects** them on trigger conditions (path/symbol/semantic/event/
temporal cues), with zero false alarms across 138 compaction cycles. Coding-agent domain. **Verdict: ADAPT
(low), harness line — design confirmation, no build.** This is the memory-side twin of the Kamradt
ontology point and our sentinel principle: **load-bearing context must be harness-injected on deterministic
triggers, never left to agent volition.** Direct application: in the depth-budget lane's Schema-class revise
loop (g50t/m0r0/sk48), the EWM contract + certificate state should be re-injected by the harness on a
deterministic cue (level change / revise-loop entry), not stored where the agent must remember to fetch it.
One sentence into the lane design doc; nothing else.

**No new harness-evolution items.** 2607.12227 remains the governor; its GitHub

## Executable world models / plan-execute-verify [line 3]

**Validate the Dream Before You Trust Its Verdict: Admissibility for World-Model Simulators**
([arXiv:2607.07196](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.07196), Jul 8) — from safety-critical simulation practice
(VV&A/SOTIF): proposes an **admissibility ladder (L0–L4)** a world model must climb before its simulated
verdicts count as evidence; key empirical result is a **ranking reversal — the model best on visual
fidelity was worse on action-following robustness.** Autonomous-driving domain, no ARC. **Verdict: ADAPT
(low-medium), EWM certification line.** Complements 2606.24842: where that paper says certificates are
*transition-local*, this one says the *acceptance procedure* should be a graded ladder and that
**surface-fidelity metrics do not predict action-conditional correctness** — precisely why our EWM contract
certifies against **history-replay of transitions** (action-following) and never against frame similarity.
Fold one line into contract v1.1 §certification framing ("admissibility-before-verdict; action-following
over surface fidelity, cf. 2607.07196"). No build.
