## 4. RESIDUAL PRICING — and it is NOT worth a slot

With discard gone, the only surviving effect of 60 → 150 s is **fewer control-returns per acting
turn (≈4.05 → ≈1.62)**, saving per-invocation re-entry overhead: the system prompt, user prompt and
upscaled frame image are re-sent, plus a `_trim_messages_for_context` pass. That saving is
**input-side**, and the bundle runs `--enable-prefix-caching`. It does **not** touch the 13.4%
output-side tail, which is where C3 claimed its win.

**A counter-effect the seal never priced, and it points the other way.** Every game ends `gave_up`
on the 7,920 s per-game clock (198,142 s / 25 = **7,925 s/game**, 25/25). When that clock fires
mid-invocation the whole in-flight invocation is abandoned. **A 150 s window abandons up to ~2.5×
more in-flight work than a 60 s window, and it does so in 25 games out of 25** — not occasionally.

**Conclusion:** the expected effect is small, **of ambiguous sign**, and cannot be attributed to
the mechanism C3 was sealed on. Against the certified comparator (field floor lc 28 + Arm A base
lc 30 ⇒ **mean 29.0, pooled seed sd 2.80**), this is far inside noise. **NOT WORTH A SLOT.**
