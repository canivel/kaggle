## Harness-evolution — TWO new items, one is the loud R17 flag [line 4]

**Rethinking the Evaluation of Harness Evolution for Agents**
([arXiv:2607.12227](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.12227v1), Jul 14) — **THE flag for today.** Empirical
critique of the entire "evolve a scaffold with test feedback, then report on the same benchmark" pattern —
i.e. the methodological silhouette of the Schema 99%-public claim. Two findings, both load-bearing for R17:
  - **Wrong baseline.** Harness evolution is itself iterative search; unless compared to test-time-scaling
    baselines (parallel sampling / sequential refinement) at *matched* compute+feedback budget, you cannot
    tell whether gains are "better design" or "just more search."
  - **Overfits the task set.** When search and eval share tasks, gains are instance memorization. On
    **held-out** Terminal-Bench 2.1 tasks, evolved harnesses give only **+0.6 avg** and do **not
    consistently beat simple test-time scaling.**
  **Verdict: ADAPT (high), harness+gate line (4).** This is the peer-style formalization of our
  generalization-first stance and it directly instruments **R17's gating decision**: (i) our
  `runs/null10` control is precisely the "matched-budget test-time-scaling baseline" this paper says every
  harness change must beat — **keep the sentinel-v2 / EWM-v1.1 gate as beat-null10-on-held-out, never
  beat-baseline-on-the-dev-games**; (ii) it is the strongest external reason to weight **held-out /
  semi-private** signal over public-25 signal when deciding what to seal today. Fold a one-line citation into
  the R17 gate rationale: *"harness changes must beat a matched-budget null baseline on held-out tasks
  (2607.12227), not merely improve on the tuning set."* No build; it's a gate-policy citation.

**TTHE: Test-Time Harness Evolution** ([arXiv:2607.08124](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08124), Jul 9) —
maintains a *population of candidate harnesses*, refines them via an agentic proposer reading execution
traces, a judge selects on execution-derived signal; **no weight updates, no gold labels, no separate
adaptation model** — all adaptation is programmatic around a frozen LLM. Demonstrated on text-to-SQL /
competitive-programming / SWE / DS-coding / tool-use (not ARC, no world-model/replay/certification).
**Verdict: PARK (in-regime but not liftable this cycle).** It is exactly our no-weight-update Kaggle regime
and its "propose→judge harness variants from traces" loop echoes our KAOS metaharness — but it offers no
ARC-specific or certification mechanism, and 2607.12227 (same window) is the corrective that says raw
harness-evolution gains may be search-not-design. **Read 2607.12227 as the governor on 2607.08124.** Note
for the KAOS metaharness backlog; no R17 action.

## World-model certification — one new item on the exact EWM pivot [line 3]

**World Models in Pieces: Structural Certification for General Agents**
([arXiv:2606.24842](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.24842), Jun 23) — proves **general agents are not universal**
(worst-case analysis is uninformative), and proposes **structural certification**: a *transition-local*
framework that certifies reliability **per-transition** (error bound O(1/n)+O(δ)) rather than globally, using
"deep compositional goals" to filter which transitions the world model is trustworthy for in long-horizon
planning. **Verdict: ADAPT (medium), EWM certification line (3).** This is the theory under our Jul-22
"certification-as-resync" pivot and it sharpens today's holdout-collapse reading: **certificates are
transition-local, not model-global** — which is *exactly why 10/11 resolver certificates dropped on holdout
while only sb26 survived.** A model-global "the EWM is certified" claim is the wrong object; the right object
is a *set of per-transition certificates*, each valid only where its history-replay held. Concrete R17 use:
frame EWM contract v1.1's certification field as **per-transition validity (with its O(1/n) sample-count
caveat), and treat holdout certificate survival (sb26) as the transition-local certificate that generalized**
— not as a failure of the whole contract. Aligns cleanly with certify-against-history → BFS-in-sim (BFS is
only sound over transitions that carry a live local certificate). No build; folds into the v1.1 §certification
wording being sealed today.
