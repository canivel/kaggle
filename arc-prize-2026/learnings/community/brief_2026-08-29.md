## ★★ SECOND — THE "CHEAPEN THE DECISION" CLOSURE FROM YESTERDAY IS TOO WIDE, AND THREE INDEPENDENT SOURCES SAY SO

Yesterday's handoff #2 read: *"BURN NO SLOT ON MAKING DECISIONS CHEAPER — the family is now closed on two independent instruments."* That conclusion is **correct for the evidence it rests on and wrong for the scope it claims**. Both instruments measured **truncation**:

- Thuitanium R35 [V-doc]: an **output-token cap** — *"a cap at 8,192 saves 0.98% of output; at 12,288 it saves nothing at all"*; `duckv9`'s 768-cap scored 0.22 by cutting the tool call.
- Our own 08-27 commit (`18c8cd3`): **context trimming** at 8192 — **4.9× worse per action** (81 calls to finish one action vs 27 for six).

Neither measured **substitution** — replacing raw carried history with a *compacted artifact*. Three independent sources landing since yesterday all point at exactly that, and one of them is quantified:

1. **[EXTERNAL, V-doc] ARC-AGI-Arcade (Agno, released 2026-08-25, open source at `github.com/agno-agi/arc-agi-arcade`).** The agent keeps a **per-game manual** of *"mechanics, hazards, hypotheses"* and consults it on later levels. The decisive number: **Gemini-3.7-Flash went from a cold 37.33 RHAE to 96.42 RHAE — above the 95.4 human baseline — when *"seeded with `gpt-5.6`'s manuals"*, while using 3× FEWER tokens.** A weaker, cheaper model reached frontier performance on a compacted memory artifact **at a third of the token cost.**
2. **[EXTERNAL, V-doc] Symbolica AI "Arcgentica"** — orchestrator/subagent split where subagents return **compressed textual summaries**, *"constraining context growth and allowing the system to maintain a higher-level plan without exceeding context limits."*
3. **[DISCUSSION, V-doc] Ivan Martin Valle, topic 737230, 2026-08-28 18:43** — the only new comment field-wide: *"I spent a lot of time optimizing for level completeness, but then I realized **action** efficiency was scoring better… once I started optimizing for **token efficiency**, then I started moving in score."* **Price this honestly: he is #491 at 1.33 on 40 subs** [V]. He moved +0.15 today. It is a third arrow in the same direction, not an authority.

**Why this is decision-grade for us and not a rerun of a dead idea.** Our binding constraint is measured and not in dispute: 675/675 games died on the 7920 s clock (`feedback_decision_budget_binding`), and our own 08-28 decomposition put **99% of that clock in token generation** (`d6c5de3`). Truncation fails because it deletes information the next call must re-derive. Compaction is the opposite operation — it pays once to make the re-derivation unnecessary — and the Arcade result is the first evidence anywhere that it moves **both** dials at once (37→96 RHAE **and** 3× fewer tokens). Our harness already produces a carried world model at **96.3% delivery** with no measured behaviour change (mech-C), so the open question is unchanged; what is new is an external existence proof that the compacted-artifact version of it is worth 2.6× RHAE on a *weak* model.

**The generalisation trap, stated before anyone walks into it:** the Arcade's headline warm run seeds manuals for the **25 public games**, and the LB set is **110** [V-doc, OverfitOracle]. Precomputing public-game manuals offline is textbook public overfitting and `feedback_arc_generalization_first` forbids it. What transfers is the **within-run** mechanism — a manual that accumulates across the six levels of a game the agent is seeing for the first time.
