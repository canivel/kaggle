**(3) We downloaded and read it. It is a complete executable-world-model agent on the 27B rail, and its rationale strings are measurements of *our own engine*.** Loop: **Observe → Edit → Plan → Act**. The agent writes `policy.py` exposing `predict(state, action)`; `verifier.py` replays **every** observed transition cell-for-cell; only a **certified** policy (matched == total, total > 0) is allowed into the BFS planner. Five design decisions carry explicit, load-bearing rationale [all V-doc, read from source]:

| mechanism | the rationale, verbatim-in-substance | why it lands on us |
|---|---|---|
| **Retrodiction, not rollout** | each transition predicted from the *observed* prior frame, never from the policy's own output — *"a rollout would compound one early error into total failure and report the same thing for a nearly-right policy as for a hopeless one"* | makes `matched/total` a **gradient**. Our EXEC-WM charges a **BREAK** on mispredict and latches fallback at 3 — a hard threshold where Polyphony deliberately built a soft one |
| **Sticky policy deadline `0.55`** | abandons uncertified model-building for direct play at 55% of the game clock, *"because the likeliest failure of this arm is not a bad policy, it is an elegant policy and zero actions played"* / without it *"the likeliest outcome is '25 games, 0 actions, score 0', which teaches nothing"* | **an engineered answer to our ★★★ `feedback_decision_budget_binding`** — 675/675 games died on the 7920 s clock at 12.8% of design turns |
| **Bootstrap probe (`probe_cap 12`)** | plays every action class once at level start **before the model sees a token** — *"**Q3.8's median to full coverage was nine moves, and in five games it never tried every class at all**"* | a measured claim about **our exact engine**, and *"a trace log missing an action class cannot certify any policy"* |
| **Anti-gaming verifier** | the replay harness is **inlined into the sandbox**, *"unreachable from the workspace… so the agent cannot loosen the comparison it is judged by"* | direct answer to `feedback_guard_never_fired` — a gate the subject can edit is not a gate |
| **Thin feedback (`verify_max_diff_cells 20`)** | 20 cells, not the full diff: *"a full 64×64 frame diff is 4096 numbers the model **has been shown not to use on demand**, and it would crowd out the notes that carry the reasoning forward"* | independent convergence with `feedback_visible_vs_hidden_channel` |

Its caps are attributed to a named prior system: *"**NOOA's two hanging games** hung in improvised searches without exactly these caps"* — NOOA is NVIDIA's, **85.1%** on the community board. `metrics.py` is a instrument design we could adopt outright: `retrodiction_accuracy_over_time`, `time_to_first_certified_policy_s`, `planned_actions` vs `improvised_actions`, `policy_deadline_hit` — written *"so that a score of zero is still a result."*
