**(2) `thtennant/arc3-duck-v25` (08-25 12:47) ships `winframe`, and its thesis is a harness-level frame-layer defect that lands directly on EXEC-WM's verifier.** Claim [V-doc]: `GameState.frame` returns `Frame(data=self.raw.frame[-1])` — *"the final visible frame."* On an ordinary step `raw.frame` holds one layer and that is the board. **On the step that COMPLETES a level it holds two: layer 0 is the terminal board of the level just cleared, layer 1 is the opening board of the NEXT one.** `.ascii`, `.segmentation` and every graft read the last layer — so at the single most informative instant in the game the agent is handed the next level's board and told it is the current one.

**We tested this ourselves this morning, offline, against `data/human_replays/*.jsonl` — six games with recorded level wins. It reproduces exactly** [V, ours]:

| game | layers on ordinary steps | layers at level-win steps | prev-board vs **layer 0** | prev-board vs **layer[-1]** | layer[-1] vs NEXT step's board |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| lp85 | **1** (416/424 steps) | **2** (7 of 8 wins) | 288 cells | **1505 cells** | 93 cells |
| ar25 | **1** (570/579) | 3 (7 of 8 wins) | 105 cells | **692 cells** | **0 cells — exact identity** |
| cd82 | **1** (110/138) | 15–16 (6 of 6 wins) | **1 cell** | **410 cells** | 51 cells |

Layer 0 is one small edit from the previous board — it *is* the result of the winning action. Layer[-1] is a wholly different board and on ar25 is **bit-identical to the next step's board**. Every level-win step in all six games (ar25, cd82, lp85, sb26, sc25, tu93) carries extra layers. The claim is confirmed on our rail.

**This retires a conclusion from our own 08-24 brief.** That brief recorded busyaprime's film-strip finding and answered: *"our lineage already reads `raw.frame[-1]` — audited, no defect."* That audit covered **animation strips mid-level**; it did not cover **level transitions**, where reading `[-1]` is precisely the defect. Correct the record before anything is built on it.

**Why it plausibly bears on the 1.05 — stated as a hypothesis with its own test, not a conclusion.** EXEC-WM's settled-frame reader takes the last layer (`f[-1] if f.ndim == 3`, `duck_eval/execwm/ewm_smoke.py:328`). PHASE P predicts, acts, then compares the settled interior. **At the level-clearing step the comparison is against the next level's opening board — a guaranteed clean mispredict, which the prereg charges as a BREAK, and `MAX_BREAKS_PER_LEVEL = 3` latches fallback.** If real, the arm would be penalised hardest at exactly the moment it succeeded, and `levels_cleared_by_plan` (the D3 conversion instrument) would under-count. **The artifact settles it in minutes:** the per-game reports record breaks vs events and `levels_cleared_by_plan`. If breaks cluster on level-completing steps, this is an INFRA-class instrument defect and **the prereg's decisive-kill clause must not be fired against a mis-instrumented run.**
