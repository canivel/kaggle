## 1. OpenAI: "How enabling two settings tripled our scores on ARC-AGI-3" (GPT-5.6 Sol)
- OpenAI blog, 2026-07-30 — https://openai.com/index/how-two-settings-tripled-our-arc-agi-3-scores/
  (403 to our fetcher; corroborated via https://explainx.ai/blog/openai-arc-agi-3-retained-reasoning-compaction-july-2026)
- Takeaway: GPT-5.6 Sol jumps **13.3% → 38.3% public set** (new frontier SOTA, past Opus 5
  High 30.2%) from two *harness settings*, zero model change: (a) **retained reasoning** —
  private reasoning preserved across turns so plans survive between actions; (b)
  **compaction** — summarize/compress older context instead of rolling truncation (their old
  harness silently dropped oldest messages at ~175K chars). Same model used **~6x fewer
  output tokens** while scoring higher.
- **Verdict: ADAPT (principle; post-gate lane, not a mid-gate injection).** This is the
  strongest published confirmation of our latent-state-audit thesis: the binding constraint
  is *state continuity in the harness*, not model capability or token volume. Both settings
  are model-agnostic, in-context, and zero-cost — compatible with zero-cloud-budget, and the
  6x token cut is directly friendly to the 9h wall. Boristown gate A/B is preregistered, so
  no change to the active arm; instead file "compaction-not-truncation + cross-turn plan
  retention in the duck harness history manager" as an R23 agenda item / candidate next
  preregistered arm. Also update leaderboard priors: frontier public reference is now 38.3%.

## 2. Rethinking Inference-Time Scaling in Local Computer-Use Agents
- arXiv:2607.28573 (2026-07-30) — https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28573
- Takeaway: Studies resource-constrained local/open-weight agents; failure modes are
  repetitive trajectories, stalled execution, false success declarations. Core finding:
  additional inference-time compute yields diminishing returns and *changes* failure modes
  rather than fixing them; contextual scaling stabilizes trajectories but saturates fast;
  temporal scaling reduces stalls without raising success.
- **Verdict: IGNORE (corroborating, no build).** Independently supports two of our sealed
  calls: (a) simplicity-wins / don't token-scale a broken lane, and (b) the 72B livelock was
  not going to be fixed by more sampling or longer budgets — compute scaling changes the
  failure mode, it does not repair a structural one. Cite in a17_error_model if revisited.
