## 5. Interpretation — the fix worked, the mechanism still loses

1. **Engineering verdict: clean.** Region-aware eviction ran 2,617 times with zero fallbacks, zero pin
   yields, zero brickings; RETAIN stayed off; hygiene gate rejected ~54 % of raw FACTs; empty-gate and
   reserve-only-when-earned behaved exactly per spec. Nothing here is a bug story.
2. **The v1 root-cause theory is now weakened.** v1's screen attributed harm to (toxic digest ×
   blind batching). v2 removed both channels and got a *worse* M1 (−0.32 vs −0.20, lc 14 vs 17,
   p 0.056 vs 0.234) with *normal* action economics. Either the residual mechanism (eviction pressure +
   standing ~950-token digest + 1000-token reserve on 81 % of trims) is itself harmful on this stack, or
   between-run variance at n=1 seed is large enough to swamp these mean shifts — the war baseline itself
   has never been replicated at a second seed.
3. **M3 is the one real positive** and it is causally coherent: the never-elided refuted list is exactly
   the payload that reduces re-proposal. But it purchases a digest that saturates its budget forever, and
   the M2 split shows the run spent ~half its eviction relief (plus the reserve) carrying it. The lane's
   own LightMem framing says the win should have come from relief with digest≈0 — that configuration was
   never tested in isolation.
4. **sc25 stayed at 0/2 with every proposed cure applied.** Its harm is not the digest content and not
   batching. Under compaction it consistently gets fewer turns (49, 67 vs war's 101) at much higher
   tokens/turn — long-generation crowd-out is the remaining suspect channel common to v1 and v2.

## 6. Recommended next step for the lane (decision belongs to R24, not this screen)

The prereg pause is in force: **no further v2 pushes as-is.** A v2 seed-2 run of the identical mechanism
risks the lane's terminal FAIL to confirm a screen that already failed at near-significance. If the lane
gets one more slot, the highest-information single push consistent with K4's spirit is the **pure-eviction
sub-arm: v2 with the digest render disabled entirely** (empty-gate forced; reserve never applied; eviction
and capture unchanged) — it is the LightMem regime the prereg's own M2 prediction names and was never
observed; it isolates the only untested factor (standing digest + reserve) from eviction itself, and M3's
seed-1 win vs loss under that arm would cleanly attribute whether the refuted-list payload or the budget
relief carries the plan-signal effect. If instead the lane is closed, the M3 reversal (−4.6 pp, p = 0.012)
is the result worth keeping: hygiene-gated, never-elided refuted lists measurably reduce re-proposal — file
it for the next memory-lane design even if this compaction vehicle dies.
