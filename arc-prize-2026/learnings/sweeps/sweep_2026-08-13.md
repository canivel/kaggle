### §1.1 — D1 in full: the only ADOPT, and it is verified rather than believed

**His claim.** "The Tufa Duck harness only captures persistent memory updates from visible outputs,
but the model might put the updates in hidden reasoning instead… Tufa's prompt does not explicitly
tell the model to put the updates in visible output." Follow-up comment, **using Qwen, not DeepSeek**:
2,615 responses, 2,580 with tool calls, **1,723 tool-call responses with hidden reasoning and zero
visible content — 66.8%**, all 25 games affected, 29–151 calls per game.

**Mechanism, verified in our own tree (not inferred):**

- `duck_eval/taaf_bundle/src/ARC3-Inference/inference/agent/tool_agent.py:1973` and `:2007` both call
  `self._update_summarized_knowledge_from_assistant(content)` — **`content` only**. `reasoning` is
  captured six lines earlier (`:1966–1969`) into the transcript and into
  `assistant_message["reasoning"]`, and is **never parsed for the labels**.
- `_update_summarized_knowledge_from_assistant` (`:1180`) → `_extract_scientist_note` (`:338`) is the
  only writer of `self._summarized_knowledge`, and `:1311` injects that block into the prompt on every
  turn. It is the state that survives the 30-turn `_persistent_history_messages` cap — i.e. it *is*
  our long-horizon memory.
- The prompt does ask for the labels in the visible channel (`:1325`, `:1995`: "*If you include
  assistant text before a tool call, keep it short and use it to update the world model. Helpful
  optional prefixes are `World model:`, `Goal model:`, …*") — but only as an *optional* nicety.
- Thinking is **on** for us: `inference/framework/kaggle.py:331–334` launches vLLM with
  `--default-chat-template-kwargs '{"preserve_thinking": true}'` **and** `--reasoning-parser qwen3`;
  `configs/inference.json` sets `"analyzer": {"thinking": true}`; `utils/openai_compat.py:78` passes
  `chat_template_kwargs={"enable_thinking": true}`. (The `enable_thinking: False` at `kaggle.py:350`
  is the smoke-test payload only, not the agent path.) So `reasoning_content` is split out of
  `content` on every single agent turn on our rail.

**Our own measurement — and this is where his headline is wrong.** Counted over three full 25-game
transcript pulls in `runs/kernel_pulls/{animation_v1, p1_v1, effnote_v1}/transcripts`:

| pull | turns | THINKING present | ASSISTANT present | **THINKING-only (no visible)** | label in ASSISTANT | label in THINKING | **label LOST** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| animation_v1 | 1,478 | 1,451 | 496 | **955 (64.6%)** | 301 (20.4%) | 32 (2.2%) | **10 (0.7%)** |
| p1_v1 | 1,519 | 1,498 | 354 | **1,144 (75.3%)** | 217 (14.3%) | 26 (1.7%) | **25 (1.6%)** |
| effnote_v1 | 1,444 | 1,418 | 450 | **968 (67.0%)** | 240 (16.6%) | 15 (1.0%) | **14 (1.0%)** |

His **symptom** statistic replicates almost exactly — 64.6–75.3% vs his 66.8%. His **inference** does
not. An empty visible channel is only a memory loss if the model *would have* written a labelled
update there, and our Qwen puts the labels in the visible channel **10–20× more often** than in the
thinking channel. Actual loss is **0.7–1.6% of turns**, or **3–10% of all label-bearing turns**.
*This is `feedback_audit_the_instrument` applied to someone else's instrument: 66.8% is a denominator
he never conditioned on.*

**Verdict ADOPT, but only the narrow form.** Parse the labels out of `reasoning` **only when `content`
is empty**. That is exactly the 0.7–1.6% population, and in that population there is no competing
visible statement to overwrite, so the obvious harm channel — importing an abandoned mid-thought
hypothesis over a considered visible one — is excluded by construction. ~2 lines, zero tokens, zero
extra LLM calls, no prompt change (so `feedback_prompt_is_noise` is not engaged, and his own patch
notebook — which changes the *prompt* to demand visible updates — is **not** what we take: a prompt
demand for visible notes costs output tokens on every turn, and under the wall-clock cap Brüggen
measured, every token is paid for in moves).

**Constraints on the ADOPT:** (i) it **must not buy its own build** — fold it into the next scheduled
build as a rider; at ~1% of turns it cannot justify one of our two monthly build slots on its own.
(ii) Pre-register the endpoint as **delivery, not score**: count of recovered label-updates per run,
band `[0.5%, 3%]` of turns, from the measured 0.7–1.6%. A score endpoint on a 1%-of-turns change is
un-powered against our ledger sd of 0.15 and would be an instrument we already know lies to us.

## §3. WHAT, IF ANYTHING, CHANGES THE PLAN

**One thing changes, and it is small, cheap and verified. Everything else is reprioritisation.**

**Changes: D1, the reasoning-channel memory drop.** Our shipped harness silently discards world-model
updates that Qwen writes into the thinking channel, and we now have this from three directions —
a competitor's report, the code path read line-by-line in our own tree, and 4,441 turns of our own
transcripts. The narrow fix (parse labels from `reasoning` **only when `content` is empty**) is ~2
lines, costs zero tokens and zero LLM calls, has no prompt component, and its harm channel is excluded
by construction. It rides the next scheduled build as a rider with a **delivery** endpoint banded at
`[0.5%, 3%]` of turns. **It does not buy a build and it is not a score claim** — our own numbers say
the recoverable population is ~1% of turns, not the 66.8% the post's headline implies.

**Does not change: the strategic frame.** Nothing this week touches the harness-diff conclusion. No
1.4+ team posted; the two substantive posts come from a 1.23 team; **cstl moved 1.4 sigma further away
(2.52 → 2.70) and the forum still has not noticed.** The public record continues to contain no
explanation of the top of the board, which means the plan remains what `what_the_field_runs` set out:
raise the **mean** of our draw distribution, because the private prize is a twin-of-2 and we are
already marginally above the field's single-draw mean and behind only on draw count.

**Reprioritisation, not new work.** R1 and R3 point the same way from opposite directions —
**executive beats advisory, and harness gains come from moving work out of the model into deterministic
code, not from asking the model to think or notice more.** R1 supplies an 11,700-trajectory negative on
exactly the advisory-note class our effnote lane lives in. That is now the standing tie-break for arm
selection, and it demotes any future proposal whose mechanism is "give the model a place to write
things down and hope it uses it." R2 adds one deterministic field (the step ordinal) to whatever stub
the compaction lane eventually emits, and stands as A22's second R2-candidate — **still not two builds'
worth of evidence, still no build bought.**

**Nothing here reopens anything closed.** Exec-sims/certified world models stay closed (S1 fired, E1 =
0/13). Animation stays killed (K-A3, plus the author's own +1.4%/p=0.92 null). No item in this sweep
proposes "let the model notice it is repeating itself" — the closest, R6, is retrieval sophistication
and is IGNOREd for assuming a querying agent we have measured we do not have; RedundancyBench's 24.88%
remains the standing answer if it comes back. **Zero cloud spend proposed. Zero prompt changes
proposed.**

## §4. OPEN QUESTIONS

1. **Does the ~1% recoverable population concentrate?** The loss rate is 0.7–1.6% of turns overall, but
   if the lost updates cluster on the turns immediately *before* a compaction eviction — i.e. exactly
   when the note is the only thing that would have survived — the effective value is much higher than
   the headline rate. Cheap to answer offline from the transcripts we already hold; worth doing before
   the rider ships, because it decides whether the endpoint band is right.
2. **Is `content`-empty the right gate, or should it be `content`-carries-no-label?** A turn with a
   short visible sentence that contains no `World model:` label is currently excluded by our narrow
   gate but has the same "nothing was captured" property. Widening it re-opens the override harm
   channel. Answerable from the same transcript pass.
3. **What does k-candidate persistence cost per turn in tokens?** The single number that decides whether
   the BeliefMem ADAPT survives its 900-char budget. Readable from Feng's published notebook without a
   build.
4. **cstl at 2.70, z ≈ +5, 24 submissions, zero forum trace, zero artifacts.** Unchanged from 08-12 and
   still the largest unexplained fact on the board. If the public 1.44–1.62 band really is order
   statistics on the stock harness, 2.70 is not on that distribution at all and something structural is
   being done that no public kernel contains.
5. **Does our Qwen ever emit a label it then contradicts?** R2's gist finding and the override harm
   channel in D1 both assume the thinking stream contains abandoned hypotheses. We have not measured
   how often a `World model:` line is superseded within the same run. If it is rare, the D1 gate could
   safely widen; if common, the narrow gate is load-bearing rather than merely cautious.
