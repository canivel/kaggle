both arms' `transcripts/*_p0.txt`): extract `[THINKING]` blocks per `analysis_step`; sentence-split;

## 5. Mechanism read — the digest is not empty, it is *toxic*

### 5.1 Is the digest content-empty? No — but it starts empty and fills with the wrong thing

Distribution over all **1449** `COMPACTION` event lines:

| field | min | p25 | median | p75 | max | mean | % zero |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `facts` | 0 | 0 | 6 | 22 | 132 | 14.1 | **28.8 %** |
| `refuted` | 0 | 0 | 0 | 36 | 182 | 23.8 | **51.6 %** |
| `digest_tokens` | 101 | 388 | 670 | 757 | 777 | 575.6 | 0 % |
| `retained_reasoning_msgs` | 2 | 5 | 7 | 11 | 19 | 8.0 | 0 % |
| `evicted_msgs` | 2 | 3 | 3 | 5 | 9 | 3.8 | 0 % |
| `evicted_chars` | 4387 | 6767 | 8103 | 10031 | 34250 | 8887 | 0 % |

- first 100 events: mean facts **0.34**, refuted **0.53** → the early-run `facts=0 refuted=0` lines are a
  warm-up artefact, not the steady state.
- last 100 events: mean facts **29.6**, refuted **41.7**.
- 244 events (16.8 %) carry a digest with *neither* a fact nor a refutation (pure action-count header,
  `digest_tokens≈101`) — those are literally "rolling cut with extra steps", but they are a minority.

**So the HeuristicExtractor is not extracting nothing.** The failure is upstream of volume.

Three observations, all load-bearing:

1. **Hedged speculation is laundered into asserted FACTs.** F56/F58/F60/F61 are the model's own
   *"Actually, I think…" / "Wait, maybe…" / "But then, why…"* musings — mutually **contradictory**
   click-models — promoted to FACT records and stamped *do NOT re-verify*. This is worse than eviction:
   eviction forgets, this **freezes a wrong world model and forbids re-testing it**.
2. **Records are truncated mid-sentence at ~110 chars** (`…not just the c`, `…reverts. Can`), so even
   the genuinely-factual ones are unusable as facts.
3. **The digest self-ingests.** sc25 `FACT F5` is literally
   `FACT F5: "FACT F3: Clicking on the purple dot had no effect…"` — the model echoed the injected
   digest into its own world model, and the extractor re-harvested its own output on the next eviction.
   The prereg §2 claim *"never compound, never self-ingested"* holds only for the message-level strip;
   it does **not** hold for the round-trip through the model's text. This is a real design hole.

**ft09 (Δlc −1).** The refuted content is *summarised into a count and discarded*:


77 refuted hypotheses collapse to the string "+77 more". **The refuted set — the exact thing M3
measures and the exact thing compaction was built to preserve — is rendered as a cardinality, not as
content.** That is the direct explanation for M3 showing zero reduction: nothing re-proposable was
actually re-injected.

**s5i5 (Δlc −1).** `ACTIVE H145` / `H144` — 145 live hypotheses, `(+18 more transfer; 111 more ot…)`.
The extractor treats every sentence containing a hedge as a hypothesis, so the ledger explodes, then
the renderer truncates head+tail (F1–F6 … F57–F62) and drops the middle. The digest is a
**random 12-record window over a 145-record pile of speculation.**

### 5.3 The second, independent harm channel: turn economics

Retained reasoning re-enters the prompt each turn and inflates generation (+34 % tokens/turn). Under a
fixed 7921 s wallclock this trades *observe-plan-act cycles* for *thinking*, and the agent compensates
by batching more actions per turn:

- **sc25: 49 turns / 291 actions (5.9 acts/turn)** vs war **101 turns / 184 actions (1.8 acts/turn)**.
  Half the feedback cycles, 58 % more actions, and its digest still reads `max_level_seen=1 level_ups=0`
  where war-seed-1 cleared 2 levels. It ran a 12-action `RIGHT×9` blind batch at step 258.
- Same pattern in sk48 (49 vs 91 turns, 785 vs 266 actions) and re86 (38 vs 67 turns).
- The 1000-token digest reserve is subtracted from the 31744-token trim budget unconditionally, so the
  live window shrinks 3.2 % even on the 244 events where the digest carried nothing. Minor next to the
  above, but it is a pure loss.

### 5.4 One-line read

The mechanism engaged perfectly and injected the wrong payload: the HeuristicExtractor promotes the
model's hedged, mid-sentence-truncated musings to "do-not-re-verify FACTs" while collapsing the actual
refuted set to a count — so compaction froze wrong world models instead of preserving refutations, and
retained reasoning spent the wallclock on thinking rather than observe-act cycles.

## 6. If the arm is revived (prerequisites before any seed-2 slot)

1. **Extraction precision, not recall.** Reject any candidate FACT containing a hedge marker
   (*maybe / I think / actually / wait / perhaps / but then / why*) or ending mid-sentence. A digest of
   3 true facts beats 60 contradictory ones under a "do not re-verify" header.
2. **Render refuted content, not `+77 more`.** Refuted hypotheses are the payload M3 measures; budget
   the 1000-token reserve to REFUTED first, FACT second, action-effects third.
3. **Soften the directive.** *"do NOT re-verify"* on machine-extracted, unvalidated records is the
   harm amplifier. Downgrade to "previously observed — treat as prior, not proof."
4. **Break the self-ingestion loop** by tagging digest-derived text and excluding it from re-extraction
   (currently only the message-level marker strip exists; the model round-trips it as prose).
5. **Consider splitting the flag** despite single-flag discipline: RETAIN and COMPACT have opposite
   failure modes here (RETAIN costs turns; COMPACT costs correctness). K4's retain-only sub-arm is the
   sanctioned route and is now the more informative one.
