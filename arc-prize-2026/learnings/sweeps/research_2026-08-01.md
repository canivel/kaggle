## 1. Tycho: Active Abstraction with Programmatic World Models for ARC-AGI-3  ⭐ HEADLINE
- arXiv:2607.28287 (2026-07-30; Lehmann, Aioanei, Vahdati / NIMI-research) —
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28287 — code: github.com/NIMI-research/Tycho (open source)
- Takeaway: An **inference-only** coding-agent that builds a *game-specific programmatic world
  model* through interaction, then plans against that executable hypothesis. Core idea =
  "active abstraction": the agent decides when to construct / repair / use / bypass the model
  based on value-vs-cost during play. Also does structured history management, separating
  actionable observation frames from animation / level-complete / game-over frames.
- Reported (25 public test games, 183 levels): **GPT-5.6 Sol and Opus 5 both hit 100.00 RHAE
  (all 183 levels); Opus 5 used 61% fewer scored actions than the aggregate human baseline;
  Claude Opus 4.8 = 88.49 RHAE.** This is a *second* independent frontier result blowing past
  the 38.3% public number — but on the **public** games with a **tool-using research harness**
  (code execution / model-builder delegation), i.e. NOT the Kaggle-legal restricted-prompt /
  no-external-tools setting the private competition enforces.
- **Verdict: ADAPT (principle only; strong prior update, NOT a portable build).** Two takeaways
  land on our lane: (a) the *world-model-as-executable-hypothesis + frame-type filtering* is a
  concrete instantiation of "state continuity beats raw capability" — same thesis as the
  OpenAI compaction result, from the opposite (programmatic) direction, and it validates
  keeping a world-model/banking arm alive behind the compaction arm. (b) BUT the RHAE=100 is
  earned with code-execution tools + multi-model orchestration on *public* games — explicitly
  out-of-bounds for the private Kaggle track (restricted prompt, no external tools). So it does
  **not** transfer to a submission, and the frame-filtering trick is the only piece that's
  even harness-portable. Do NOT touch the sealed boristown arm. File under R23 as: "programmatic
  world-model + observation/animation frame separation" as a post-compaction candidate arm, and
  update the leaderboard prior note (frontier can saturate public games with tools; our lane is
  the harder no-tools private set). Watch the repo for a distillable frame-classifier heuristic.

## 2. Plans Don't Persist: Why Context Management Is Load-Bearing for LLM Agents
- arXiv:2606.22953 (2026-06-22; new-to-us) — https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.22953
- Takeaway: Direct empirical backing for our latent-state-audit thesis and a sharp caution on
  the compaction arm. Plan signal decays **4.1x within a single action–observation step**
  (Llama-3.1-70B; 12.4x on HotpotQA); naive plan eviction costs **34.7 pts** on ALFWorld. Key
  warning: their "probe-gated re-surfacing" of evicted plans **does not recover** the loss —
  "context management is load-bearing, but plan protection alone is not enough."
- **Verdict: ADAPT (design constraint on the compaction arm).** This is the most important
  methodological note for the *next* preregistered arm: it says a naive "compact-not-truncate"
  diff that merely keeps a plan summary visible may under-deliver, because agents don't
  internalize plans as state and re-surfacing alone doesn't restore signal. Implication for the
  compaction-arm prereg: measure plan-signal survival directly, and pair compaction with
  *retained-reasoning* (the OpenAI cross-turn private-reasoning setting) rather than summary
  re-injection alone. Sharpens the arm spec; changes nothing on today's seal.

## 3. MemHarness: Memory Is Reconstructed, Not Replayed
- arXiv:2607.28272 (2026-07-30) — https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28272
- Takeaway: Argues verbatim **replay of retrieved experience causes negative transfer** when
  contexts don't align; agents should critique+reconstruct past experience conditioned on the
  present state before acting. Trained end-to-end with GRPO (RL).
- **Verdict: IGNORE for build (ADAPT as a caution on the banking lane).** Requires RL training —
  budget-incompatible, so no build. But it's a second source (with Plans-Don't-Persist) warning
  that our shelved EWM+banking/replay idea, if ever revived, should reconstruct-to-context not
  replay-verbatim. Reinforces why the latent-state audit still blocks naive banking. Citation
  for a17_error_model / banking-lane record.

## 4. Context-management cluster (Self-GC, Addressable Recall Compaction) — compaction-arm refs
- Self-GC 2607.00692 (07-01): side-channel planner proposes fold/mask/prune actions on indexed
  context objects; 91–95% no-impact while cutting prefix tokens 10–15% (heuristic baselines
  78–87%). — https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.00692
- Addressable Recall Compaction 2607.25066 (07-27): **training-free**, append-only ID-addressable
  log; replaces old observations with compact references the agent can dereference *losslessly*
  without re-running tools or similarity retrieval. — https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.25066
- **Verdict: IGNORE (compaction-lane reference shelf; ARC-2607.25066 is the notable one).** Both
  are the "smarter than truncation" end of the compaction spectrum. Self-GC needs a side-channel
  planner (extra moving part, questionable under the 9h wall). Addressable Recall Compaction is
  the interesting zero-train option: lossless pointer recall > lossy summarization, and it's
  training-free so it's at least budget-legal — but it presupposes a tool-observation log the
  duck harness doesn't currently expose, so it's a design reference for the compaction arm, not
  a drop-in. Logged so the compaction-arm record shows the zero-train pointer variant alongside
  the OpenAI summary-compaction variant.
