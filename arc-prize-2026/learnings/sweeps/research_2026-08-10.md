## 2. New items — ADOPT

### 2608.07077 — "Transformers Struggle to Use Their Emergent World Models: Revisiting the Tower of Hanoi, and the Illusion of Thinking" (08-07)

**ADOPT (mechanism evidence) — lane (a), state externalisation.** This is the item of the week.

Small Transformers trained from scratch on Tower-of-Hanoi solution traces develop an **emergent
world model**: a linearly decodable, geometrically faithful representation of the puzzle state space
(the Sierpiński triangle), shown by interpretability probes to be **causally involved** in solving.
The authors then apply the same probes to two frontier reasoning models — **Qwen3.6-27B** and
DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-32B — and find both **encode the Sierpiński world model near-perfectly at
the end of the prompt** and nevertheless **fail the majority of tasks beyond 3 rings**. Probing at
successive planning stages locates the failure in the **decaying representation** of the world
model, and causality is established by showing that **injecting the prompt-time representation at
inference partially recovers performance**. Their framing: *"current Large Reasoning Models build a
world model, and then lose it."*

**Why this matters to us more than anything else in the batch.**
1. **It is measured on Qwen3.6-27B — our exact substrate.** Every prior lane-(a) support has been
   frontier-closed-model (Argus/GPT-5.5, 2608.06370's 14 closed models). The weak-model hole that
   R24 §3(a) evidence-against (i) kept open has, for the *first* time, a same-backbone datum on the
   correct side.
2. **It states lane (a)'s thesis as a measured causal mechanism, not an analogy.** Our proposal
   argues the agent should hold state in an external, persistent artifact rather than in the
   transcript. This paper says the 27B *already builds* the right internal state and then *loses it
   over the reasoning horizon* — i.e. the deficit externalisation targets is **maintenance**, not
   construction. That is a sharper and more falsifiable claim than the one we had.
3. **It reframes our own failure taxonomy.** `transcript_forensics.md` ranks a persistent hypothesis
   ledger as fix #1 on grounds of observed hypothesis churn. "Builds it, then loses it" is precisely
   what hypothesis churn looks like from the outside.

**Three honest discounts, all load-bearing — do not let this be over-quoted.**
- **Tower of Hanoi is not ARC-AGI-3.** It is a fully-observed, deterministic, single-goal puzzle
  with a known state space. ARC-AGI-3's state space is *unknown to the agent*, which is the entire
  difficulty. The transfer is an assumption.
- **The intervention is in activation space, not text space.** They inject a hidden-state
  representation at inference. We cannot do that: our rail is a frozen fork behind a vLLM server
  with no activation access, on zero budget. **Text-space externalisation is the assumed analogue of
  their intervention, not the intervention they tested.** Anyone quoting this at R25 must say so.
- **"Partially recoverable" is qualitative in the abstract** — no recovery percentage is given. Do
  not attach a number to it.

**Cheapest test under our constraints ($0, zero pushes):** fold a **representation-decay proxy** into
the already-planned transcript-forensics pass on `runs/kernel_pulls/war_eval_v1/`. If the paper's
mechanism holds in text, we should observe the 27B's *explicit* state descriptions degrading with
turn index within a level — measurable as re-statement drift, contradiction against earlier
statements, and re-derivation of facts already established. That is a decay curve, computable
offline, and it is the closest observable we can get to their probe. Pair it with the
`namespace_reuse_rate` estimator validation that R24 §5.3 already owes before any P1 screen — same
transcript pass, one job.

## 3. New items — ADAPT

### 2608.07169 — "Agent Memory Distillation: Empowering Small LLM Agents with Hierarchical Teacher Memory" (08-07)

**ADAPT (strong) — lane (b), arm P3.** Weak-model memory datum **#2**, and much stronger than #1.

**Training-free.** Transfers structured knowledge from a large teacher agent to a small student via
three memory tiers built from successful *teacher* trajectories: **Workflow** (task-level strategy,
injected proactively at task start), **Subtask** (concrete behavioural examples at intermediate
granularity, also proactive), and **Function** (per-function calling conventions and common
pitfalls, retrieved **reactively on tool-calling errors**). Four students at **4B–8B parameters**,
GPT-5-mini as teacher: **+27.2pp on AppWorld, +11.2pp on BFCL V3, +3.4pp on ToolSandbox**,
consistently beating existing memory baselines. Ablation: **Subtask memory contributes the largest
gains**, and **4B students benefit most**.

**What it changes for us, concretely.**
- **R24 §5.6 respecified P3 as two-timescale memory** (consolidate at first level-clear, then
  refine-not-rewrite). AMD says **three** tiers, and that the *middle* one — concrete behavioural
  examples, not the top-level policy — carries the gains. If P3 is built as authored, it may be
  built at the wrong granularity.
- **It supplies the L4 consult gate we have been hand-waving.** The reactive tier fires on a
  **tool-calling error** — an observable, zero-inference trigger. Yesterday's RPS report
  (733865) gave a working prior for a consult gate on design grounds only; this gives a *measured*
  one, with an ablation behind it.
- **Feasibility, and why it is in-bounds.** AMD needs a large teacher, and we cannot call a frontier
  model from inside a Kaggle kernel on $0. But **R24 ratified workstation-LLM authoring as in-bounds
  of the zero-budget rail** — so an **offline-authored teacher memory shipped as a static dataset
  artifact** is exactly the AMD shape, costs $0, and needs no inference budget at run time. That is a
  genuinely new, actionable design option that did not exist before this morning.

**Discounts.** Tool-use benchmarks, not interactive games. The effect is strongly domain-dependent
(+27.2pp down to +3.4pp — the floor is nearly noise). Teacher/student *compatibility* is a stated
moderator, and GPT-5-mini → Qwen3.6-27B is a different pairing than the one tested. The gains are
measured on 4B–8B; our 27B is above the band where they report the largest benefit.

### 2608.07429 — "TEPA: Revoking Stale Memories for Conflict-Robust Language Agents" (08-07)

**ADAPT — P3 store schema.** The strongest measured negative-result-about-memory in two sweeps.

TEPA makes **validity an explicit state of memory**: observations are stored as **keyed precedents**,
and an active precedent is **revoked** when fresh evidence contradicts it under the same key, while
revoked history is **preserved for audit** and can be re-promoted later. The measured result is the
part that matters: under controlled hidden-regime drift over **50 seeds**, at full reversal,
**append-only memory (0.210) and last-write-wins (0.210) both fall BELOW no-memory-at-all (0.309)**,
while TEPA holds **0.950** — and the pattern reproduces under real file-backed executable drift
(append-only 0.203, no memory 0.298, TEPA 0.950). On clean `MemoryAgentBench` SH-6k, TEPA only
**matches** a strong last-write-wins cache.

**Why this is aimed directly at P3.** Our P3 as specified is an accumulating store of cross-level
observations in a setting where the agent's own hypotheses are routinely **falsified mid-game**.
That is TEPA's exact failure mode, and the measurement says an append-only store under reversal is
**worse than having no memory** — which would look, on our rail, like P3 failing K3′ and being
recorded as the *fifth* mechanism negative for reasons that have nothing to do with the mechanism.
It also gives the **refuted-hypothesis content** — dropped as a standalone micro-arm at R24 §2 — a
principled home: **revoke-with-audit inside the store**, not a digest injected into the window
(which is the A22 design that failed). MERIT (2608.05906) reached a compatible conclusion via
dual-polarity storage; TEPA reaches it independently with a **far larger measured effect**.

**Discounts.** `MemoryAgentBench` is fact-consolidation and preference-update streams, not
interactive game state. The abstract names **no backbone and no parameter count**, and does not
explicitly assert training-free status — so this is a *schema* recommendation, not a weak-model
feasibility datum. And the clean-benchmark parity result is honest evidence that **the entire gain
is a drift/reversal effect** — if in-game hypotheses were rarely reversed, TEPA would buy nothing.

### 2608.06984 — "HarnessSafe: Evaluating Safety Across Persistent Carriers in Agent Harnesses" (08-07)

**ADAPT (risk control) — lane (a), sandbox risk-class trigger.**

328 executable cases across **seven persistent-carrier families** (memory, skills, tools, shared
artifacts, …), evaluated on "most mainstream agent harnesses", each specified as a **Persistent-Risk
Lifecycle** traced from entry → persistence across carriers and system boundaries → later benign
trigger → observable violation. Findings: **containment is carrier-specific and depends jointly on
the harness AND the model backend**, and **end-to-end attack-success rates cannot distinguish
lifecycle progression patterns** — you need trace-based, multi-stage evaluation to see where a chain
is stopped.

**Relevance:** R24 ratified a **sandbox risk-class trigger** uncontested, but specified it from first
principles. Our P1 persistent namespace *is* a persistent carrier in this taxonomy, and this is the
first published carrier taxonomy we could specify that trigger against rather than inventing one.
The "ASR hides where the chain stopped" finding also rhymes with our own week: **an aggregate
statistic that conceals the stage at which something failed is exactly what K3 was.** Second
persistent-carrier security paper in the same batch (see 2608.06862 under IGNORE) — this is becoming
a named risk class, not a one-off.

**No efficacy content, no build.** Design/spec input only.

### 2608.07440 — "Blast Radius" (08-07) — **heavily de-rated**

**ADAPT (design pointer only).** First compaction-theory item in **four** sweeps, landing on the day
after A22's death record was vacated.

A predictive memory-management layer for agentic coding. **NECROPHORESIS** enables **reversible
eviction** by archiving evicted context **verbatim, byte-exact**; **Recurring Dead Matter (RDM)**
identifies and buries repeatedly-occurring transcripts. Reversible eviction is formalised over a
"Polish context space" connecting context entropy to *resurrection probability*. Across **seven
OpenAI models**: **17–26% token reduction**, lowest overflow rate among tested policies, byte-exact
reversible.

**The de-rating, and the number the authors bury.** *"Of 450 buried bodies, 378 were recurring dead
matter and zero were recalled."* **Zero recalls out of 450** means the reversibility channel — the
paper's headline novelty — was **never exercised in the reported experiments**. What is actually
demonstrated is a **compression** result, not a **reversibility** result. Compounding this:
closed models only (seven unnamed OpenAI models, no open weights, no parameter counts), **no named
benchmark in the abstract**, an idiosyncratic self-coined framework ("Algosophy", "Polish context
space", "buried bodies"), and no stated affiliation. Under the **standing provenance de-rating rule
(R24 §5.3.2)** this is at best `[SR-adjacent]`; treat the 17–26% as unreplicated and
self-benchmarked, with 2608.06196's measured **up-to-44pp** self-authored-benchmark inflation as the
governing prior. **Official-LB counterweight, stated separately as the rule requires:** the only
externally-adjudicated ARC-AGI-3 figure remains arcprize.org's **Claude Opus 5 at 30.2%** (dated
2026-07-24) — nothing in this paper touches that board, and none of its numbers may appear in the
same sentence as a Kaggle score.

**Why it is filed rather than ignored — two reasons, both narrow.**
1. **The "compaction field is quiet ⇒ A22 closure unchallenged" premise (R24 §5.2) is now doubly
   withdrawn.** It was already vacated on internal grounds yesterday (the death record was an
   artefact of a broken gate against a lucky baseline). The external premise it also rested on —
   three consecutive silent sweeps — no longer holds either. This does **not** resurrect A22; it
   removes one of the two supports the closure was standing on.
2. **It names the affordance A22's design lacked.** A22 evicted context and injected a lossy digest.
   Blast Radius evicts context and keeps a **byte-exact archive with a recall channel**. If A22 is
   ever re-screened under K3′ — an open question for today, not a decision — *that* is the variant
   worth screening, not a re-run of v2.1.

## 8. What this changes, and what it does not

**Lane ranking: NO CHANGE.** (a) > (c) > (b) stands. But the *evidential balance* moved on two axes.

1. **Lane (a) got its first same-backbone mechanism evidence** (2608.07077). Every prior support was
   frontier-closed-model. The R24 §3(a) weak-model objection is not *closed* — Tower of Hanoi is not
   ARC-AGI-3 and the intervention was in activation space — but it is no longer *unaddressed*, which
   is a real change in the state of the argument and should be said plainly at R25.
2. **Lane (b)/P3 got a specification, not a promotion.** AMD (tiering + a measured reactive trigger)
   and TEPA (revocation as a first-class memory operation) between them say that the P3 currently
   specified in R24 §5.6 is **the wrong shape in two identifiable ways**: wrong granularity
   (two-timescale where the evidence favours three, with the middle tier carrying the gain) and
   missing a validity lifecycle (append-only under reversal measures **worse than no memory**). P3's
   *rank* is unchanged; its *design* now has two concrete, free, text-only corrections available.
3. **Nothing found today bears on ρ̂, on K3′'s calibration, or on the m ≥ 3 baseline cost.** The
   day's binding constraints are internal and no external work touches them.
4. **One methodological echo worth stating.** HarnessSafe's finding that an end-to-end aggregate
   (attack-success rate) *cannot reveal at which stage a chain was stopped* is structurally the same
   defect the campaign found in K3 this week: a single conjunctive summary statistic that concealed
   which leg was firing and why. Independent field, same lesson.
