## Summary (2 sentences)

The brief's evidence discipline is genuinely strong (censoring-aware LB reads, sealed prereg, instrument-defect confession), but its two load-bearing quantitative moves — the "+0.17-class" re-anchoring of the Q38 prior and the "control arm did not move" inference — are both statistically unsound under the max-censoring the authors themselves document, and the sealed Q38 read is now running with no stated power analysis against the collapsed prior. ## Objections

**[MAJOR] The +0.17-class re-anchor is built on a selectively censored sample of its own dateable points.** By the brief's own narrow dating rule (ΔSubmissionCount == 1 ∧ Score improved), there are at least *three* dateable post-release points: aRc (binary relation) +0.74, Ya Xu +0.17, FOYSAL +0.00 — and the +0.74 is presented in §1b as an *attribution finding* while being silently excluded from the prior re-anchor two paragraphs later. A prior anchored on {+0.74, +0.17, 0.00} is a very different object from "+0.17-class, inside ledger noise." Additionally, FOYSAL's 0.00 and Ya Xu's +0.17 are max-censored observations: observed Δbest is a lower bound on the draw-level effect (a +0.17 mean shift can easily produce Δbest = 0 on one draw when the incumbent best is already a high-percentile draw). The fix is mechanical: state the full dateable set, model the censoring explicitly (P(new draw > banked best | shift δ) under the team's inferable dispersion), and re-derive the prior — or withdraw the re-anchor and say "prior is unidentified from LB data."

**[MAJOR] "The control arm did not move" is n=1 per team under max-censoring and cannot bear the weight placed on it.** Using our own ledger as the noise model (s ≈ 0.156), a team banked at 1.62 whose per-draw mean shifted up by +0.17 still fails to beat its own best on a single draw with substantial probability — plausibly ~40–60% depending on where their mean sits relative to their max. Two flat single draws from MindsAI and Tufa are therefore only weakly informative against the engine-swap story, yet the brief bolds this as "the load-bearing observation" and lets it drive the shared-regime read. Compute the actual likelihood ratio P(flat | swap, δ=+0.17-class) vs P(flat | no swap) before calling this line load-bearing; my back-of-envelope says it is close to 1.

**[MAJOR] Q38 v2 is running against a sealed read whose power under the collapsed prior is unstated — and OQ3 asks the panel to adjudicate this *after* the push.** The CONFIRM-2× ≥ 32 / REFUTE-2× ≤ 25 thresholds were presumably calibrated when the prior was "2×." Nowhere is the mapping given from a +0.17-LB-class true effect to expected *level counts*, so we cannot know whether REFUTE-2× at δ=+0.17 is a refutation of the engine claim or a foregone conclusion of an underpowered arm. This is answerable now, before data lands: publish the level-count distribution under H0 (Qwen3.6 baseline runs already on disk) and the implied level-count shift for δ ∈ {0, +0.17, +0.74}, and pre-commit the disposition table (effect-refuted vs power-limited) as a function of the observed count. If that calculation shows REFUTE-2× is the modal outcome even under δ=+0.17, the arm should be re-scoped before slot 2 is spent, sealed read or not — sealing protects constants from post-hoc motion, not from a pre-data power audit.

**[MAJOR] OQ1's monotonicity probe has no estimand, no statistic, and no pass/fail rule — it is not yet a falsifiable experiment.** I endorse the reframing direction (2608.12959/12321's "information present, consumer's criterion doesn't use it" is exactly the right diagnostic class, and it is squarely in my field), but "is the agent's action-selection criterion monotone in what scores?" is currently a slogan. Before this goes on the rail, it needs: (a) the precise pair (candidate-ranking signal, realized score contribution) whose rank correlation is being tested, (b) the trajectory sample and its selection rule, (c) a pre-registered threshold (e.g., Kendall τ with a CI excluding some null band) and (d) what action follows from each outcome — noting that on the papers' own account the *fix* does not transfer (no explicit CEM cost to swap), so a confirmed non-monotonicity must name the intervention it licenses or it is a diagnostic with no consumer, the very pathology the papers describe.

**[MAJOR] OQ5 identifies the campaign-fatal gap and the brief supplies nothing against it.** Public max frozen at 1.33 for 29 days, −30 ranks in two days, both gaps widening, and the only arm in flight (Q38) now carries an author-admitted prior "well inside our own ledger noise" against a bar of 1.0826 — which, note, is a *mean-of-4* bar, so even a true +0.17 shift (mean → ~1.11) clears it only marginally and stochastically. The brief's honest accounting makes the conclusion unavoidable: there is no mechanism on the rail with an expected effect that clears the bar with margin. A daily brief at this stage of a 78-day runway must contain at least one concrete candidate-mechanism proposal (the cstl-family ceiling ≥ 2.70 on our own artifact family is the standing existence proof that headroom exists in *configuration*, not just engine), or state explicitly that the campaign strategy is now "await Q38 + instrument hygiene," which the panel should then evaluate as such.

**[MINOR] Verifier fixes applied mid-flight are asserted, not verified.** Both step-3 defects were "fixed in the verifier, not the artifact," and fix 2 is claimed "strictly stronger" — but no regression test is cited for either, and the Q38_VETO-aware assert is exactly the kind of gate whose failure mode (silently passing a poisoned incumbent) is invisible until it matters. Given that today's own generalisable lesson is "a gate suite that aborts on its cheapest check never runs its most load-bearing one," the fixed suite should also be *reordered* load-bearing-first (engine-attachment check before cosmetic byte checks), not just patched; the brief adopts the lesson rhetorically but not structurally.

**[MINOR] OQ2's pre-registered null prediction needs a falsification arm to be science rather than insurance.** Pre-registering "raising the 31,744 ceiling will null" is cheap and I support it, but as stated it is unfalsifiable-in-practice for the routing hypothesis: a null confirms it, and a positive can be attributed to power or confounds. State now what observed effect size at the raised ceiling would count as *refuting* the routing reading of 2608.12321 for our substrate.
