## Objections

**[MAJOR] The sealed Q38 read is now known-underpowered against the re-anchored prior, and the proposal declines to compute this.** The read thresholds (CONFIRM-2× ≥ 32 levels, REFUTE-2× ≤ 25) were sealed when the working prior was "2×." Section 1b re-anchors the prior to +0.17-class — inside your own ledger noise (s = 0.1563) — and then asks the panel to "decide the disposition now" without supplying the one number needed to decide it: P(read ≥ 32 | true effect = +0.17) under the arm's level-count variance. This is computable *today*, CPU-only, from the frozen fork's level-count distribution across your 33 filler draws (you have the per-draw level counts on disk; map score→levels and simulate the 2× read). If that probability is below ~0.5 — which the ledger dispersion strongly suggests — a REFUTE is nearly uninformative about the engine claim and you must say so *in the prereg annex before the kernel completes*, or the sealed read becomes a coin-flip ritual. Deciding "before the data" is the right instinct; deciding without a power calculation is not deciding, it is postponing with extra steps.

**[MAJOR] The "+0.17-class" re-anchor treats a censored max-increment as an effect estimate — this is statistically wrong in both directions.** Ya Xu's +0.17 is a single draw's increment over a banked best, i.e., a *truncated* observation of the new distribution (you only see the part of the new draw that exceeded the old max); FOYSAL's 1.61 → 1.61 is *right-censored* — his post-swap draw could have been 0.9 or 1.60 and the board shows the same nothing, so "FOYSAL drew +0.00" is not a data point, it is an unobserved value with an upper bound. Averaging one truncated point and one censored non-point into a "+0.17-class" prior is exactly the best-of-N pathology your own adopted paper (2608.13087) warns about. The correct statement is: the two dateable observations are *consistent with* effects anywhere from ~0 to well above +0.3 depending on where the swapped-engine mean sits relative to each team's banked max. Rewrite the prior as an interval with the censoring model explicit, or drop the re-anchor and admit the prior is uninformative.

**[MAJOR] Question 5 is the plan's actual center of mass and it arrives with zero proposed content.** By the brief's own numbers: promotion bar 1.0826, gold line 1.65, prize line 1.98, gap widening for two consecutive days, 78 days to deadline, and the prize is the *private twin of two selected submissions* (state-of-campaign §A) — meaning even a CONFIRMed +0.17-class Q38 swap lands the *mean* around ~1.10, still 0.5+ below gold. The entire rail (Q38, LoRA canary, the proposed monotonicity probe) is calibrated against the 1.0826 internal bar, which measures "better than our own frozen fork," not "competitive." A daily brief that documents −30 ranks in two days and then asks the panel an open-ended "is anything aimed at it?" without a candidate answer has identified a FATAL strategic gap and filed it as a discussion item. I require the revision to include at least one concrete artifact hypothesis with a mechanism-level reason it could clear ~1.5 (not 1.08) — the cstl trajectory (1.59 → 2.70 on *your* artifact family) proves the family ceiling permits it, so "no such artifact exists" is not available as an excuse.

**[MAJOR] The "control arm is the load-bearing observation" claim overweights two single draws against a max statistic.** MindsAI and Tufa each spent *one* draw and failed to beat their own banked bests (1.59, 1.62) — bests that are themselves maxima over dozens of submissions, plausibly ~+2σ above their per-draw means. Under a true +0.17 mean shift, the probability a *single* draw clears such a max is modest (order 30–50% by your own ledger's geometry: our mean 0.94, max 1.33 ≈ +2.5σ; shift the mean +0.17 and one draw still clears 1.33 well under half the time). So Δ/draw = 0.0000 on n=1 per team is weak evidence against the shared-engine story, not "the line that matters." The caveat paragraph is present but the framing ("load-bearing," "opposite of what a commodity-engine story predicts") contradicts it. Quantify: state P(observe both flat | engine swap with +0.17 shift) before calling this observation dispositive of anything.

**[MINOR] The Q38 read has an undefined dead zone at 26–31 levels.** CONFIRM ≥ 32, REFUTE ≤ 25: a result of 26–31 maps to no verdict, and given the re-anchored prior that zone is arguably the *most probable* landing region. Pre-register its disposition now (presumably "inconclusive, no promotion, no second slot") or the ambiguity will be resolved post-hoc, which is the thing this campaign's process exists to prevent.

**[MINOR] Open Question 1's "monotone in what scores" probe is under-specified as a measurement.** For an LLM policy with no explicit value function, "is the action-selection criterion monotone in what scores?" has no unique operationalization: monotone in *what* (logit of chosen action vs. counterfactual score-delta? rank correlation over the action set at each step?), on *which* trajectories (successful only? the selection is confounded), and with what null distribution? I support the CPU-only diagnostic direction — the two ADAPTed papers earn it — but as written this is exactly the class of under-specified consumer-side claim that produces an unfalsifiable "the objective is broken" narrative. The revision should state the estimand, the statistic, and the pre-registered threshold that would count as "not monotone" before it becomes a rail item.
