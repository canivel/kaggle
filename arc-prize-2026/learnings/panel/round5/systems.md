## Summary (2 sentences)
The proposal's subtract-and-reallocate thesis is well-evidenced and its kill discipline is genuinely improved over v4, but the compute accounting that everything hangs on — the $68 R2 cost model, the 30 h/wk Kaggle quota ledger, and the 80-window allocation — is either ungrounded or internally contradictory. Worse, the flagship free lever (L1 reallocation, EV +0.10–0.30) is in direct tension with the proposal's own headline finding that death mode is "STUCK, not out-of-time," meaning marginal compute reallocated to progressing games has no demonstrated mechanism to convert into score.

## Objections

**[MAJOR] The $15–25 R2 cost estimate has no serving-cost provenance and ignores the treatment's own token inflation** — 3 seeds × 25 games ≈ 75 games; at the proposal's own numbers (~140 actions/game, 435–650 generated tokens/action, growing per-action contexts implied by the "quadratic RHAE" observation), that is millions of generated tokens plus heavy prefill re-processing. No GPU SKU, $/hr rate, measured $/game from the null10 production runs, or parallelism factor is stated. Critically, every candidate intervention (BFS hybrid, verify loop, capability router) *adds* tokens/action — the very quantity shown to inflate 25–46% under v1/v2 — so pricing the treated arm at null-arm cost is optimistic by construction. If the true cost is $40–60/run, the "$45 covers retry OR confirmation" contingency evaporates and R2 becomes a one-shot with no retry. Required evidence: measured wall-clock and $ per null10 seed×25-game run on the intended RunPod SKU, times a token-inflation multiplier for the treated arm.

**[MAJOR] No Kaggle quota ledger — "free" is asserted, never audited** — The plan stacks daily submission windows, R1 smoke tests, preflight runtime tests, and weekly sentinels onto 30 h/wk, without ever stating the wall-clock of one scored 25-game run or one smoke run. If a scored run consumes participant-side notebook hours (typical for agent competitions) at even 4–9 h/run, near-daily windows alone exceed 30 h/wk and the entire submission policy is infeasible; if scoring is organizer-side, say so and account only for smoke/preflight. This is the single cheapest thing to fix — one table: hours per run type × frequency vs. 30 h — and its absence in a plan whose thesis is "compute allocation is the binding constraint" is ironic.

**[MAJOR] L1's EV contradicts the proposal's own "STUCK, not out-of-time" finding** — Evidence item 3 says only 9/126 good runs were still progressing in the final 15% of wall and "extra budget buys little"; L1 then claims freeing ~20% of compute for progressing games is worth +0.10–0.30. Both cannot be fully true: if progressing games are token-starved, reallocation pays; if they stall on a capability wall (the median good run burning 59% of tokens on one unfinished level says exactly this), the freed 20% is poured into the same grind. The +0.10–0.30 EV needs a mechanism: show, from null10, the score-vs-marginal-budget curve for the 9 still-progressing runs, or the fraction of level clears occurring in the last quintile of per-game budget. Without that, R1 — the plan's main free track — may be a wash and the levers-only floor (+0.25 local) is fiction.

**[MAJOR] The 80-window budget is internally contradictory and 45+ windows are unaccounted** — The enumerated gated candidates (R1: 2–3, R2 confirmation: 2–3, explore-min: 2–3, plus retries) total ~10–15 windows, yet 60 are allocated to "gated candidates"; either 45 windows of candidates exist that the plan doesn't name (violating pre-registration) or the bucket is padding. Simultaneously, "non-sentinel redraws of unchanged builds banned from draw #7" collides with "remainder: redundancy draws of the best promoted build" — redundancy draws of a promoted-then-unchanged build are precisely banned redraws. Also 1/week sentinel over Jul 14–Sep 30 is ~11 windows, not 8. Publish a reconciled per-week window ledger before R0 exits.

**[MAJOR] The window gate is underpowered at its own printed uncertainty, and R2 confirmation authority is ambiguous** — At the σ̂ CI upper endpoint (0.213), the 2-window SE is ≈0.17, so the promote threshold Δ ≥ +0.12 is <1 SE: promotions near threshold are coin flips, and false-*promote* risk (shipping a null or negative component into the R3 stack) is never quantified, only the 30% false-kill. Separately, R2 says "Pass → confirmation via 2-window rule" (free) but also that the $45 reserve covers "one confirmation sweep" (GPU) — which instrument is the promotion authority, and what does the GPU confirmation buy that windows don't? Pre-register the false-promote probability at both CI endpoints and resolve the confirmation-path ambiguity.

**[MINOR] Local RHAE scoring on RunPod hardware doesn't transfer for wall-clock-penalized metrics** — RHAE is stated to penalize wall-clock quadratically; local A/B hardware (RunPod GPU, whatever SKU) differs from Kaggle serving hardware, so the R2 *secondary* gate (paired RHAE delta ≥ 0) is measured under the wrong clock, and scheduler levers are exactly the change class where this bias is largest (Risk 4 admits this but doesn't fix it). Mitigate by expressing the secondary gate in action-count/token terms, or normalize local wall-clock to Kaggle via the null10-vs-official paired runs already in hand.

**[MINOR] Sentinel drift detection is statistically blind at n=1/week** — With σ̂ ≈ 0.074 (CI to 0.213), a single weekly draw cannot distinguish version drift from draw noise unless the drift exceeds ~0.15–0.4; given 15/24 games were version-unstable in July, define a per-game (not aggregate-score) drift statistic from the sentinel's event log, which has far more power at the same window cost.

## Questions for the authors (numbered)
1. What is the measured wall-clock and dollar cost, on the intended RunPod SKU, of producing one null10 seed (25 games)? Show the arithmetic behind $15–25 including the expected tokens/action inflation of the treated arm.
2. Does a scored Kaggle submission window consume participant-side notebook GPU hours from the 30 h/wk quota, and if so, how many hours per run? Provide the weekly quota ledger including smoke/preflight runs.
3. From null10: what fraction of level clears occur in the final 20% of a game's per-game budget, and what did the 9 still-progressing runs score per marginal 10% of budget? This is the direct test of L1's +0.10–0.30 EV.
4. Enumerate the candidates behind the "60 gated-candidate windows," and reconcile the redundancy-draw allocation with the draw-#7 redraw ban.
5. Which instrument is the R2 promotion authority — the free 2-window rule or the $45 GPU "confirmation sweep" — and what decision does the GPU spend change?
6. If the treated R2 run costs 2× the estimate (plausible given +25–46% tokens/action precedent), what is the pre-registered fallback: no retry, or cannibalize the confirmation?
7. What is the false-promote probability of the 2-window gate for a true Δ=0 component at σ̂ = 0.213, and how many such components can enter the R3 stack before the stack's expected value goes negative?
