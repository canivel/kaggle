## Summary (2 sentences)
This is a statistically literate proposal — pre-registration, paired controls, printed CIs — but three of its load-bearing quantitative claims (redraws-are-dead, the reallocation EV, and the R2 gate's error rate) do not survive scrutiny at the proposal's own uncertainty endpoints or under the correlation structure of its own data. The gating machinery is sound in spirit but is calibrated against a point estimate of σ with df≈5 and a baseline that the plan itself documents as nonstationary, so the stated error rates are decorative rather than real.

## Objections

**[MAJOR] "Redraws are mathematically dead" fails the plan's own both-endpoints rule** — The expected best-of-80 ≈ 1.10 and P(top-100 by luck) ≈ 2.8% are computed at σ̂ = 0.074 (point estimate, df≈5). At the upper χ² CI endpoint the plan itself prints (σ = 0.213), E[max of 80 normal draws] ≈ 0.922 + 2.47·0.213 ≈ 1.45, comfortably above the 1.17 cutoff; and with ft09 (26% of score, bimodal) the draw distribution is right-skewed, inflating the max further beyond the normal approximation. Since this single claim drives the ban on redraws from draw #7 and the entire 80-window allocation, it must be re-derived at both σ endpoints and with a bootstrap over the empirical (non-normal) draw distribution before the ban is adopted. As written, a strategy option was killed on a df=5 point estimate — exactly the inference style the plan elsewhere forbids.

**[MAJOR] R1's reallocation EV is contradicted by the plan's own Evidence #3** — Evidence #3 concludes "STUCK, not out-of-time: only 9/126 good runs were still progressing in the final 15% of wall; extra budget buys little." If marginal compute buys little for progressing games, then L1's mechanism — freeing 20% of compute from dead games and giving it to progressing games — has no demonstrated compute→score elasticity, and the EV of +0.10–0.30 (and hence the "+0.25 local levers" target arithmetic underpinning the whole Sep-30 rank story) is unsupported. Either the true mechanism is something else (e.g., reduced wall-clock RHAE penalty), in which case quantify *that* channel directly from null10, or supply the missing evidence: an offline replay / CPU extended-budget arm estimating score gain per marginal action on progressing games. The window gate will eventually catch a null effect, but the plan's headline arithmetic currently rests on a self-refuted assumption.

**[MAJOR] The window gate assumes a stationary, exchangeable baseline it demonstrably lacks** — Δ = (2 fresh candidate draws) − (6 baseline draws frozen in mid-July), evaluated through September, in an environment where 15/24 games were version-unstable in July and the cutoff drifts +0.02/day. Any environment shift between baseline collection and candidate gating is confounded with the build effect at exactly the ±0.12 scale the gate decides on; a 1/week sentinel draw (single draw, SE = σ̂ ≈ 0.074) can detect gross drift but cannot re-center the baseline at that precision. Fix: use a rolling baseline (k most recent sentinel + baseline draws) or gate on candidate-minus-same-week-sentinel paired differences, and pre-register how a sentinel drift alarm invalidates in-flight gates.

**[MAJOR] R2's primary gate treats 9 clustered trials as independent Bernoulli draws** — The 9 trials are 3 seeds × 3 games, and the team's own data shows per-game outcomes are near-deterministic across seeds (level-1: 10/10; level-2: 0/10). Under that correlation structure, one game becoming trivially solvable yields 3/9 in all seeds and passes the gate with effective n ≈ 3, so P(≥3/9 | p=0.1) ≈ 0.05 is invalid — the real false-pass rate against a single-game fluke is far higher. Fix: require level-2 clears on ≥2 *distinct* games as the primary endpoint, and compute the null at the game level (exceedance over game-clusters), not pooled binomial.

**[MAJOR] The rehabilitation of substrate v1 is unregistered subgroup analysis** — "v1 = +0.13 (+0.42 version-stable)" conditions on a "version-stable" subset apparently defined after outcomes were observed, and the v2 decomposition leans on a *non-significant* binomial p = 0.22 as if it were positive evidence for the "half is lottery" claim (absence of significance at n=3 is not evidence of absence of effect). This matters because v1's rehabilitated sign is the credential that lets explore-min back into R3. Treat both numbers as hypotheses: pre-register the version-stability criterion, rescore on fresh seeds, and let explore-min enter R3 with zero prior credence until then.

**[MINOR] Multiplicity across gates and additive stacking are unquantified** — Roughly 5–8 candidate families, each with a 2-SE promote threshold plus one retry, gives a family-wise false-promote probability of ~15–30% at nominal σ (worse at the upper CI endpoint), and R3 then implicitly assumes promoted deltas stack additively despite plausible interactions (two schedulers plus explore-min all compete for the same wall clock). State the expected number of false promotions, and make explicit that the frozen stacked build must beat the *vanilla-duck* fork on its own pre-registered window test, not merely the frozen-fork mean.

**[MINOR] R2's secondary no-collateral gate has near-zero power** — "Mean paired RHAE delta ≥ 0 at n=3 seeds" is approximately a coin flip against a true −0.1 to −0.2 harm (the same magnitude as the v2 pollution effect this plan just spent two weeks excavating). The downstream window gate caps the damage at wasted windows, but a per-game paired sign statistic over the ~22 non-wall games (game as exchangeable unit, per the carried-forward ethos) would give real power at the same cost.

## Questions for the authors (numbered)
1. Provide the six baseline draw values. How was σ̂ = 0.074 estimated — same build, same weeks? Does it absorb or exclude version-drift variance, and what is the evidence the draws are exchangeable?
2. Recompute E[best-of-80] and P(exceed cutoff trajectory) at σ = 0.044 and σ = 0.213 and via bootstrap on the empirical draw distribution. Does "redraws are dead" survive all three?
3. For L1: what is the measured score gain per marginal action on progressing games in null10 (or via replay)? If the mechanism is RHAE wall-clock instead, what is the quantified penalty avoided?
4. Was the "version-stable" game subset defined by a criterion fixed before observing v1/v2 outcomes? If not, will you rescore with a pre-registered criterion on fresh seeds?
5. Is the 6-draw window-gate baseline frozen from July or rolling? What is the pre-registered action when a sentinel draw deviates by >2σ̂ while a candidate gate is in flight?
6. Under game-level clustering, what is your recomputed false-pass probability for the R2 primary gate, and will you switch the endpoint to ≥2 distinct games?
7. What is the sample size behind the 0.55 difficulty ratio, and its CI? (You flag the risk; I want the number.)
8. How many total candidate gates do you expect to run by Sep 12, and what family-wise false-promote rate does that imply at both σ endpoints?
