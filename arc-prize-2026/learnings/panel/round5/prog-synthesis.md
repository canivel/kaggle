## Summary (2 sentences)
The proposal's diagnostic work is genuinely strong — the subtract-first reallocation and the killing of always-on context injection are well-evidenced — but the entire path from "hold rank" to "contest top-100" runs through R2, which is the least specified component in the document: three architecturally incommensurable interventions, no validated intermediate representations, no synthesis-quality metrics, and a local gate whose statistics are miscomputed and inconsistent with its own confirmation gate. As written, R2 is a $68 coin-flip dressed in pre-registration language, and two of the plan's headline EV claims contradict each other's evidence.

## Objections

**[MAJOR] R2 has no engineering substance and no intermediate verification gates — the only score-moving lever is a slogan.** "Duck+BFS hybrid over the object graph, stall-scoped exec-WM verify loop, or capability router" are three different multi-week research projects, and the plan commits to picking one by Aug 3 and having it built, smoked, and locally A/B'd by Aug 10. Critically, both of the first two presuppose components with measurable fidelity that is never measured: the BFS hybrid presupposes an object-graph extractor whose faithfulness to the actual game state has apparently never been validated, and an executable world model is worthless unless it predicts transitions. Fix: pre-register **free, CPU-only component gates against null10 transcripts before any GPU spend** — e.g., object-graph extractor achieves ≥X% object-identity/transition consistency on logged frames from the three grinders; exec-WM achieves ≥Y% next-state prediction accuracy on held-out logged transitions. If the world model can't predict logged history, the $68 endpoint A/B is uninformative about anything except noise.

**[MAJOR] The R2 primary gate is statistically incoherent and inconsistent with its own confirmation gate.** The ≥3/9 calculation treats 3 seeds × 3 games as 9 independent Bernoulli trials; they are not — if the intervention cracks one game's mechanic, its 3 seeds will tend to clear together, so the effective unit is the game (n=3) and P(pass | one-game-only success) ≈ 1, not the implied 0.05 null-adjacent regime. Worse, by the proposal's own arithmetic a one-grinder crack is worth roughly +0.17 local → ~+0.09 official, which is *below* the +0.12 window promotion threshold — so an intervention can legitimately pass the local gate and then be killed at confirmation by construction. Fix: make the primary gate game-level (e.g., ≥2/3 grinders with ≥2/3 seeds each), and pre-register a confirmation threshold consistent with the expected official delta of the local pass condition.

**[MAJOR] L1's EV claim contradicts the proposal's own STUCK finding.** Evidence point 3 states only 9/126 good runs were still progressing at the final 15% of wall and "extra budget buys little" — yet L1's entire value proposition is that freeing ~20% of compute from dead games converts to +0.10–0.30 by giving it "to progressing games," which the evidence says barely exist and which are otherwise stuck at level-2 walls in 10/10 seeds. Where exactly does the marginal compute earn score? Fix: before spending windows on R1, run a **counterfactual replay on null10** (free): truncate the 5 dead games at action 120, redistribute the token budget per the actual scheduler policy, and report the simulated score delta with per-game attribution. If the simulation can't produce +0.10, the lever's EV is fiction and R1's window budget should shrink.

**[MAJOR] The mechanism-forensics loop is designed and gated on the same three games — no out-of-sample test, and quiet leakage of game-specific knowledge into design.** Mining sb26/su15/lp85 transcripts, forming a hypothesis about *their* mechanics (which requires a human to know the "correct mechanic" — per-game ground truth), and then gating on level-2 clears of the same three games is a textbook overfit loop; the "no game-ID-keyed logic ships" rule protects the code but not the design process, and Nov-2 has ~55 unseen games. Fix: pre-register r11l (the fourth 10/10-L1, 0/10-L2 game, excluded from the primary) as a directional holdout — the intervention must state a falsifiable prediction for r11l before the GPU run, and an r11l result contradicting the mechanism hypothesis blocks confirmation regardless of the 3-grinder count.

**[MAJOR] null10 is treated as a frozen permanent control in an environment the proposal itself says drifted 5× with 15/24 games version-unstable in July.** Every paired comparison in R1/R2 is against a corpus collected before ongoing drift; a "delta" against a stale control confounds intervention effect with environment version change, and the weekly drift sentinel monitors the *official* pipeline, not null10's validity as a local baseline. Fix: version-pin the local environment used for null10 and all treated runs, and pre-register a null10 refresh trigger (e.g., sentinel drift > 2σ̂ or any game version bump on the grinder set forces re-baselining before any gate decision is honored).

**[MAJOR] Window-gate operating characteristics are unstable across the printed σ̂ CI, and only the final-selection criterion binds at both endpoints.** With σ̂ ∈ [0.044, 0.213], the +0.12 promote threshold is anywhere from ~2.7 SE to ~0.7 SE — at the upper endpoint the false-promote rate under a null candidate is large, and multiple candidate families (R1, R2 confirmation, explore-min, stacks, retries) tested against the same 6-draw baseline inflate family-wise false-promotion further. Printing the CI beside the decision is transparency, not protection. Fix: require the promote decision to hold at the σ̂ upper endpoint (as the freeze criterion already does), or pre-register a stricter threshold for any candidate after the first promotion.

**[MINOR] The Nov-2 fallback thesis ("game-agnostic logic compresses luck-chasers on private games") is currently unfalsifiable and carries the entire justification for the campaign if R2 fails.** It may be true, but no evidence is offered and none is planned. A cheap proxy exists: audit the public Milestone-1 winning notebook and top public kernels for game-ID-keyed logic or public-set-tuned parameters, and quantify how much of the leaders' scores comes from games absent from any plausible private distribution; pre-register that audit as an R0/R3 deliverable.

## Questions for the authors (numbered)
1. How were the ar25 p-values (0.009/0.008) computed — what is the unit of analysis and n, given the plan elsewhere bans n=3 A/B inference? If they are per-action or per-episode tests, what is the seed-level replication?
2. What concretely is the "object graph" the BFS hybrid searches over — how are objects segmented from frames, has extraction accuracy ever been measured against even one hand-labeled null10 episode, and what happens to BFS when the extraction is wrong?
3. Reconcile the R2 gates: if the intervention cracks exactly one grinder (3/9, local pass), the expected official delta (~+0.09) is below the +0.12 window promotion threshold — what is the pre-registered decision in that case?
4. What does the forensics pipeline count as the agent "stating the correct mechanic" — who supplies the ground-truth mechanic labels, and how do you prevent that human knowledge from shaping the shipped intervention beyond the three studied games?
5. What counterfactual analysis produced L1's EV of +0.10–0.30, given your own finding that non-dead runs are stuck rather than time-limited?
6. Under what conditions is null10 invalidated and re-collected, given documented version instability on 15/24 games?
7. For the "offline scorer reproduces the official gate report exactly" claim — reproduced on how many builds and versions, and does exactness survive a game version bump?
