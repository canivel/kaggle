## Objections

[MAJOR] Order-statistics EV cannot reach the 1.44 wall under the team's own variance estimate — the "R1 mechanism" is a floor-raiser, not a wall-breaker. If scored draws are ≈ i.i.d. N(0.92, 0.074) as the control band {0.82…1.02} implies, then E[max of N draws] ≈ 0.92 + 0.074·E[max of N standard normals]: N=30 gives ≈ 1.07, N=100 gives ≈ 1.10, and reaching 1.44 requires a ~7σ draw. Between now and Nov 2 there are ~110 daily draws; the headline "+0.10–0.15 EV from resubmission order statistics" is therefore consistent with the max-over-draws asymptote and simultaneously proof that this mechanism alone never touches the wall. The brief half-acknowledges this in Q4 ("grinder-cracking is the leveler") but never states the ceiling number. Action: publish the expected-max-vs-N curve under both candidate variances (0.074 and the bootstrap-implied 0.52/√2-ish figure) and state explicitly which LB target each mechanism can and cannot reach. If the true per-draw sd were as large as the bootstrap implies, the ceiling story changes completely — which is exactly why the next objection is sequenced first.

[MAJOR] Launching R2 A/B (Q1 option a) before resolving the variance incoherence (ME-NEW-11/P) commits windows of unknown power. The brief itself calls variance reconciliation "blocking" for 3-seed gates in Q3, then proposes in Q1 to start window 1 of a ≥3-window A/B tonight — before that reconciliation is done. With σ per draw ≈ 0.074, a 3-window alternating A/B detects roughly Δ ≥ 0.12–0.15 at 80% power; with the bootstrap's implied sd, it detects essentially nothing, and you will have burned ≥6 daily submission slots (a hard-capped resource) on an uninterpretable experiment. Action: make the variance decomposition a same-day precondition for flipping the flag, or pre-register a stopping/extension rule ("continue windows until CI half-width < X") so the design survives either variance answer. "Rollback is a flag" mitigates code risk, not experiment-design risk.

[MAJOR] "No lift, no harm" and "fast-submit gate live and validated" overclaim from one draw. A single draw at z = −0.16 against an n=5 control band has power < ~25% to detect the claimed +0.10–0.15 EV, so the draw is equally consistent with "warpack adds +0.12" and "warpack costs −0.10"; "no harm" is not established. What was validated is the gate mechanics (dummy parquet, RUN_HEAVY=False), not the EV mechanism — the brief conflates the two in the sentence "This is the R1 mechanism working as designed." Action: state the minimum number of warpack draws needed before "no harm" is claimable at a stated confidence (under both variance candidates), and rewrite the validation claim to cover mechanics only.

[MAJOR] Banking's core claim is now structurally unfalsifiable at the per-game level, and the proposed test is underspecified. The scored rerun is hidden, there are zero per-game artifacts from any scored run, and banking's max-over-plays upside "can't be observed directly" — yet banking remains aboard the arm being A/B'd tonight, confounding any R2 effect with banking's unknown contribution. The only remaining check, "aggregate wheel-formula reconstruction vs the 7 LB totals," has no stated pass/fail criterion, no owner, and no deadline. Action: (i) pre-register the reconstruction as a concrete test (formula, tolerance, what a mismatch implies for banking); (ii) state whether the R2 A/B compares ledger-ON vs ledger-OFF *with banking held identical in both arms* — if yes, say so explicitly; if no, the A/B does not isolate anything.
