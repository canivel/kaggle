## Objections

**[MAJOR] Q1(a) sequencing contradicts the proposal's own "blocking" designation — R2 A/B must not start before variance reconciliation.** Q3 correctly states that ME-NEW-11 "decides whether 3-seed gates have any power," yet Q1 proposes flipping `ledger+escalation` ON tonight, starting window 1 of the R2 A/B whose thresholds (P1–P5) were set under the unreconciled noise model. Under the control-implied σ̂ = 0.074, a 3-vs-3 window design has SE(Δ) ≈ 0.074·√(2/3) ≈ 0.060, so a +0.10 effect is ~1.7 SE — roughly 35–40% power at α = 0.05; under the bootstrap-implied variance (5–7× larger), power is effectively nil. Actionable fix: complete the reconciliation today (as proposed), then recompute MDE/power and the number of windows, amend P1–P5 thresholds if needed, and only then start window 1 — tonight's submission should be (b) another war-v1 draw, which is useful regardless of which variance is right.

**[MAJOR] The warpack arm has no valid control band; the reported z ≈ −0.16 is computed against the wrong distribution.** The control set {0.82, 0.89, 0.93, 1.02, 0.95} comes from the frozen fork, but the brief itself concedes warpack "is NOT the frozen fork" (Q2). Banking = max-over-plays changes the *shape* of the per-draw distribution (right-shifted mean, compressed left tail), so a z-score against the frozen-fork mean/σ tests a mismatched null. Moreover, with n = 5 the 95% CI on σ is roughly [0.044, 0.21] (χ², 4 df), so the band itself is barely estimated, and a t-statistic with 4 df — not z — is the correct reference. Actionable fix: answer Q2 affirmatively — open a separate warpack control ledger, accumulate ≥5 war-v1 draws before quoting any standardized effect, and quote t with df, not z.

**[MAJOR] The order-statistics arithmetic refutes Q4's claim that "the wall mechanism is now adopted."** Under the team's own band (mean 0.922, σ̂ 0.074), the expected maximum over k daily draws is mean + σ·E[max of k standard normals]: ≈ 1.07 at k = 30 and ≈ 1.11 at k = 100 — the 1.44 wall sits ~7σ above the band and is unreachable by resubmission order statistics from this distribution. Therefore fast-submit + order stats is *not* the wall players' mechanism as applied to this arm; the wall must reflect a materially better per-draw mean. Q4's conclusion (don't chase 1.86) may still be right, but its stated justification is quantitatively false and should be replaced by the honest version: only per-draw mean improvements (R2–R5) can close the gap; order stats buys at most ~+0.15–0.19 over the mean, ever. Publish this expected-max-vs-k curve in path_forward_v5 so the strategy rests on correct arithmetic.

**[MAJOR] The warpack "+0.10–0.15 EV" claim has no defined estimand and, as stated, is untestable by the accumulating-draws inference proposed in Q2.** If the EV comes from *resubmission order statistics* (max over more daily draws, enabled by the fast-submit gate), that gain accrues mechanically from draw count and applies equally to any arm that resubmits daily — it is not a property of warpack's per-draw distribution and no number of z/t comparisons against control will ever confirm or refute it. If instead warpack is claimed to shift the per-draw distribution (via banking), that is a different, testable hypothesis requiring the separate control band from my second objection. Actionable fix: split the claim into (i) mechanism EV = f(k, σ) — verified by the expected-max curve and the draw log, no LB inference needed — and (ii) per-draw distributional effect — pre-register a two-sample test with stated n and MDE.

**[MINOR] Briefing/document factual discrepancy on headline numbers.** The panel briefing states team best 0.43 and leader 1.56; the document states LB best 1.02 (draw #4) and leader 1.86. One of these is stale or wrong; since every gap calculation in §1b and Q4 depends on these numbers, attach submission-history screenshots or the exported submissions CSV to the next brief so reviewers can verify provenance.
