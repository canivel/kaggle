**New objections:**

**[MAJOR] NEW-1: The R1 primary lever cannot pass the plan's own promotion gate — the plan's flagship free intervention is designed to die in its own instrument.** The attempt scheduler's EV is +0.10 local → **+0.055 official**, but the window gate promotes at Δ ≥ +0.12 with SE = 0.074·√(1/2+1/6) ≈ 0.060. Under a true +0.055 effect, P(Δ̂ ≥ 0.12) ≈ 14% and P(kill, Δ̂ < 0) ≈ 18%; even granting the extra window, the modal outcome is a true-positive lever killed or stranded in the gray zone. The v2 text itself notes R2 was made "consistent by construction" (+0.19 > +0.12) but never runs the same check on R1 — the identical inconsistency my ME-M2 flagged, one level down. Fix before execution: either (a) gate the scheduler on a paired per-game local endpoint (restart-recovered clears vs null10, sign statistic over flip games) with the window test reserved for no-collateral, (b) bundle scheduler + top fork-delta into a single candidate whose combined EV clears +0.12, or (c) pre-register a lower threshold for this family with the multiplicity cost explicitly recomputed.

**[MAJOR] NEW-2: The restart EV rests on an untested exchangeability assumption and an unsourced 0.4 constant.** The +0.126 derivation assumes (i) a fresh in-run attempt is statistically equivalent to a fresh null10 seed (attempt-exchangeability), and (ii) a "depth discount 0.4" that appears nowhere in the evidence base — it is an assumed parameter driving the headline number. Worse, the flip evidence itself (16/25 games flip across 10 seeds) is potentially confounded: if the 10 null10 seeds were collected over days in a July environment where 15/24 games were version-unstable, "flips" may be version bumps *between* seed runs, not within-game stochasticity — and restarts recover nothing from version drift. Required before R1 ships: (1) verify per-game version-suffix identity across all 10 null10 seeds for the 16 flip games and recompute the flip count on version-matched rows only; (2) publish EV sensitivity at discount ∈ {0.2, 0.4, 0.6}; (3) if any logged runs contain natural mid-run resets, use them to estimate the discount empirically rather than assuming it.

**[MAJOR] NEW-3: The rolling control is self-contaminating immediately after any true promotion.** "After a promotion the promoted build becomes the control class," but the control is the mean of the **6 most recent control-class draws** — for the several weeks it takes to accumulate 6 post-promotion draws, the rolling mean mixes old-build draws that are ~0.12 lower. Every candidate gated in that window inherits a Δ inflated by up to ~+0.10, i.e., the gate's false-promote rate spikes precisely after each success, creating a cascade mechanism for the 1.5-expected-false-promotions upper bound to be realized in clusters. Fix: on promotion, either reset the control window to post-promotion draws only (accept the temporary df loss and widened SE, and say so), or offset-adjust pre-promotion draws by the gated Δ̂ estimate with the added uncertainty propagated into SE.

**[MINOR] NEW-4: The df-growth story overstates precision.** "df ≈ 15+ by September" pools sentinel, redraw, and post-promotion draws across builds and drift re-centerings; heterogeneity across eras inflates σ̂ and the effective df after each re-center or promotion resets far below the running count. State σ̂ and df per control era, not cumulatively, and have the sign-flip rule consume the era-local CI.
