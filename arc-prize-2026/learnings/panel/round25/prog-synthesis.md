### New objections

### [FATAL] N1 — The lane's kill gate is a *usage* metric with no correctness leg: the round-18 unfalsifiability charge has migrated from EWM to P1

The only pre-registered kill gate for the whole program is `namespace_reuse_rate ≥ 0.15`. A 27B model can reuse a persistent namespace at rate 1.0 while writing world models that are wrong on every transition — reuse measures *affordance uptake*, not *synthesis quality*. With L0 dissolved (P1 above), the recommended option contains **no correctness falsifier at all**: it can pass its gate, consume L1, P3 and the remaining pushes, and never once test whether the externalised artifact predicts anything. This is precisely the round-18 charge ("no world-model fidelity metric → unfalsifiable by construction") re-appearing one layer up. **Fix (free, and it reuses the existing harness):** pre-register a *paired* correctness leg alongside K4 — for every model-authored artifact produced in a screen run, replay the run's own recorded frames through it and report per-game accepted-transition-match **and** coverage (fraction of steps not returning `UNKNOWN`), with the across-stream range. Gate on the conjunction (uptake **and** non-degenerate fidelity), and pre-declare that high reuse with coverage≈1.0 and low match is a **FAIL**, not a pass.

### [FATAL] N2 — K3′ replaces a 51% false-FAIL gate with a near-100% false-PASS gate on the family it was built for, and no power analysis exists

Take §C's own numbers. `war_eval_v1/v2/v3` = 22/15/13 levels → s_base = 4.73 levels = **0.189** in Δlc units. At m=3, K3′'s PASS band is `t(0.95,2)·s_base·√(1+1/3)` = 2.920 × 0.189 × 1.155 = **0.637** — i.e. an arm that loses **16 of 25 levels** passes the calibrated non-harm gate. The document specifies type-I control and says nothing about type-II: **the minimum detectable effect is not computed anywhere**, and on the warpack family it is larger than the entire observed range of the family. A gate that cannot fail is not an improvement on a gate that always fails; it is the same information content with the sign flipped, and §C6's "no lane may be declared dead on the old gate" then also implies "no lane can be declared alive on the new one." **Required before any screen is authorised (free, arithmetic only):** publish per-family s_base, the MDE at m=3, and the m required for 80% power at the effect size lane (a) must deliver to matter (state that effect size); report whether seeds/CRN are controllable for variance reduction; and if the required m exceeds the 12–13 builds/week rail, say so and re-plan — that is a real finding, not an inconvenience.

### [FATAL] N3 — L1 is inside the recommended box, is the largest spend, has zero private-twin value by the document's own §A, and its cost benchmark does not transfer to the authoring model that will actually do it

Three compounding defects. (i) **Value:** §A says the private twin is the only currency and §B kills banking for being a public-set optimisation; a hand-migrated sim set for the 25 *public* games is exactly that object, and the parent file concedes it. (ii) **Cost:** the "~10 h / ~7 M tokens for 24 games" benchmark was a **frontier-model (opus-4-8) metered API pass**; "workstation authoring, $0" means a *different, weaker* author, so the benchmark is not a cost estimate for the work now proposed — and if it means a subscription frontier model, the no-metered-spend rule is satisfied on a technicality that should be stated openly. (iii) **Nature:** adding `UNKNOWN` is not a type migration — deciding *when a program should decline to predict* is a genuine synthesis problem, harder than writing the sim. **Fix:** either drop L1 for schema-only (my P5 fix), or, if kept, re-benchmark on **2 games with the actual authoring model** and require the measured per-game cost and the abstention-calibration quality before committing to the remaining 23.

### [MAJOR] N4 — The 52× LM-call gap is filed as a scope limit; it is arithmetically a falsifier of the synthesis half, and the calculation is free

Tycho gets 3,500 LM calls per game; we get ~67 turns per game, and §E FATAL #1 establishes ~54 s per scored action against a hard 7,920 s wall. Program synthesis is search: the currency is *propose–verify–refine iterations*, and every iteration spent writing a world model is an action not taken. So the question "how many verify-refine cycles fit in 9 h at 25 concurrent games on one GPU?" has a number, and if that number is < ~5 the synthesis half of lane (a) is dead **before any screen**, on arithmetic the team already has. §H never does this calculation and instead lists the gap as a risk bullet. **Fix (free, hours):** derive the per-game synthesis-iteration budget from `war_eval_v1/benchmark.json` latency and turn counts, and pre-register it as a go/no-go **ahead of** L1 and P1 — it is strictly cheaper than both and can kill or size the lane by itself.

### [MAJOR] N5 — The direct portability experiment is free-rail, decisive, and absent from all four options

§G states the real question as "can a state-externalizing architecture run at 27B?", then answers it only by proxy (K4 on our own harness). Tycho is **Apache-2.0** and Prime Agent **MIT**; our 27B runs behind a local OpenAI-compatible vLLM endpoint. Pointing the *unmodified* Tycho harness at our local endpoint on a handful of the 25 public games costs GPU hours and zero dollars, requires no Kaggle push, and produces the one datapoint that does not exist anywhere in the world: a weak-model ablation of a state-externalizing harness. It also cleanly separates "27B cannot do this" from "our port of it is bad" — a confound that every proposed screen in §H conflates. **Fix:** add it as the first item of the 3-week box, on 3–5 games, with pass/fail pre-registered on levels completed vs our duck baseline on the same games; if Tycho-at-27B collapses, Option 3 is triggered in week 1 for the price of ~10 GPU-hours.

§B: *"we thought we had 22/24 high-fidelity sims; we had a selection artifact evaluated on the training tuples."* §F, asset list: *"**12 exec_wm sims are fully saturated** (100% on-tuple)."* Those are the same tuples and the same selection. ### [MINOR] N7 — "Two mechanisms short of the RLM shape" measures distance to the substrate, not to the shape, and it is the load-bearing item in the viability case

Persistent namespace + durable memory give **state persistence**. Tycho's result comes from the *loop*: a builder subagent proposing programs, `/refine` self-editing, and a verifier scoring them — all three explicitly de-scoped in the same section on the 52× gap. So the honest statement is "we are 2 mechanisms from the **substrate** and the entire **synthesis loop** short of the shape," which materially weakens §G's viability case. Say it that way; it costs nothing and prevents the "2 of N" framing from being cited later as evidence the port is nearly done.
