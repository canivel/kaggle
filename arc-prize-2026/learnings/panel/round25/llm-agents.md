### New objections

**[FATAL] N1 — Option 4's pre-committed off-ramp is triggered by a gate the document itself certifies as invalid, which reproduces §C's failure mode inside the same document.** §G: "it is now *cheap to falsify* — K4 … If K4 fails, we have our answer and we stop." §H Option 4: "if K4 fails and the GLM screen format-livelocks, we adopt Option 3." §I.3: "P1's 'cheap decisive falsifier' advantage evaporated under FATAL #5." All three cannot be true. As currently scoped, a `namespace_reuse_rate < 0.15` reading measures "27B obeyed its tool schema and five prompt strings over one contradicting sentence," and cashing that in as "the campaign is structurally capped" is *precisely* killing a lane on an uncalibrated instrument — the sin §C exists to indict. **Fix (blocking, mechanical, $0):** the prereg must (i) rewrite `_PYTHON_TOOL_DESCRIPTION` (`tool_agent.py:230`), the `code` parameter schema description (`:1347`), and `prompts.py:80,82,107,113` (and `:81` if `SAFE_MODULES` widens) so the substrate no longer contradicts the mechanism; (ii) byte-audit that diff; (iii) state explicitly that no NULL reading of K4 may be entered on the record until (i) and (ii) are done. Until then Option 4 has no off-ramp, only a trapdoor.

**[MAJOR] N2 — The genuinely cheap decisive falsifier exists and is not in the plan: a prompt-only affordance probe that requires zero sandbox work.** You do not need persistent namespaces to test whether 27B will *reach for* one. Flip the six strings to *declare* persistence while leaving `run_sandboxed_python` byte-identical (runtime stays ephemeral), run the eval rail, and count cross-turn reference **attempts** — every attempt surfaces as a `NameError` traceback naming a symbol bound in a previous turn, which is trivially greppable from existing error payloads. Zero attempts across ~1,686 turns is a clean, valid substrate NULL that costs one eval run and **no** protocol rewrite, no `RLIMIT_CPU` re-accounting, no `TemporaryDirectory` change, and no exposure to the partial-binding hazard; a healthy attempt rate licenses the P1 build. This must be eval-rail-only (it is an intentionally-mismatched harness), must never touch the scored rail, and must not be gated on Δlc since it deliberately manufactures errors — report attempt-rate only. This probe answers §G's "real strategic question" for the substrate half at roughly a tenth of P1's cost and should precede it in Option 4's ordering.

**[MAJOR] N3 — The lane's load-bearing premise (portability of the shape to 27B) can be tested against the *actual open-source artifact* rather than a bespoke two-mechanism port, and the plan never considers it.** Tycho is Apache-2.0 and Prime Agent is MIT with "no ARC-specific code"; our vLLM serves an OpenAI-compatible endpoint. Pointing Tycho's released harness at Qwen3.6-27B-FP8 offline on a handful of public games — at a truncated LM-call budget, purely as a feasibility probe, never as a Kaggle artifact — directly observes whether a state-externalizing loop *functions* at 27B (does the model author a coherent `world_model.py`? does `render()` round-trip? does it abstain?), which is the exact quantity §G says is "genuinely open." Building P1+P3 into the duck instead measures our *port of an idea* and confounds "27B can't do this" with "our port was thin." **Fix:** add a §H Option 1 step-0 — a bounded (≤1 day workstation, $0) Tycho-on-27B smoke run with a pre-registered read-out (artifact well-formedness rate, abstention emitted y/n, tokens/turn), before any duck patching.

**[MAJOR] N4 — Nobody has done the amortization arithmetic that determines whether P1 can pay even if K4 passes.** Per §E, we get ~1,686 turns total, ≈67 per game, at ~54 s per scored action under a hard 9 h wall (rl-planning/systems FATALs 1–2). A persistent namespace only pays if the authoring cost of a reusable artifact is repaid by later reuse; authoring a 50–80 line world model plausibly consumes 3–8 of those 67 turns *before* any payoff, and §D's own Tycho H2 note says fidelity is necessary-not-sufficient. **Fix (free, from existing logs):** pre-register a break-even model — measured tokens/turn and s/action from `war_eval_v1`, an estimate of authoring turns from the transcript forensics, and the number of reuse turns required to repay it — and check the break-even is reachable inside 67 turns. If it is not, P1 is dominated by P3 (which has near-zero authoring cost, since the digest slot already exists) regardless of what K4 reads, and the §I.3 ordering question resolves itself.

**[MAJOR] N5 — There is no manipulation check that 27B follows *any* novel instruction on this channel, so 0.15 is un-interpretable even after N1's rewrite.** Every reading of K4 is a joint test of (substrate affordance) × (instruction compliance at 27B under a 65K-token context), and we have no measurement of the second factor on our own harness. **Fix ($0, on archived transcripts or one eval run):** insert a benign canary directive into the same channels the P1 diff will use (e.g. "begin every snippet with `# turn N`") and measure compliance rate over the full turn population. If baseline compliance on the code channel is, say, 0.4, then a K4 floor of 0.15 means something entirely different than if it is 0.95 — and the floor should be expressed as a *fraction of the measured compliance ceiling*, not as a bare constant inherited from nowhere. Without this, 0.15 is the second "inherited digit" in a document whose §C is about the first one.

**[MAJOR] N6 — §F launders a number the same document retracts.** §F lists as an asset "**12 exec_wm sims are fully saturated** (100% on-tuple)"; §B and §E FATAL 6 establish that the saturation figure was a `split=all` selection artifact evaluated on the training tuples, with on-trajectory accuracy swinging 0.026–0.879 and 0/25 implementing abstention. "100% on-tuple" is the retracted quantity wearing a hedge. Either strike it from the asset list or restate it as "12 sims fit their own training tuples; held-out fidelity unmeasured" — because §F is the paragraph that justifies "two mechanisms from the target shape," which is in turn what makes the 3-week box look feasible.

**[MINOR] N7 — "no new failure family in ~4.5 weeks" is not evidence of robustness under this plan.** The same 4.5 weeks contain zero new mechanisms on the scored rail (only frozen-fork fillers). An unchanged artifact producing unchanged failure modes is a tautology, not an asset, and it provides no prior at all for the failure surface P1 opens (bootstrap protocol rewrite, cross-turn `RLIMIT_CPU`, partial bindings). Re-word it as "no new failure family under the frozen artifact" so it is not cited as a safety margin for novel code.
