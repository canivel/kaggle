### New objections

**N1. [FATAL] K3′ is uncalibrated in the type-II direction, and on our own null10 numbers it is *looser* at m=3 than its own m=1 fallback.** null10 gives pair-sd of mean Δlc = 0.1223, hence run-level sd ≈ 0.1223/√2 = **0.0865**. Substituting into `−t(0.95,m−1)·s_base·√(1+1/m)`: m=3 → −2.920 × 0.0865 × 1.1547 = **−0.292**; m=5 → −0.202; m=10 → −0.166. So the stated fallbacks (−0.200 at m=1, **−0.190 at m≥3**) are not reproducible from the stated formula, and the formula's small-m behaviour is perverse — buying two extra baseline runs *widens* the PASS band because t(0.95,2)=2.92 dominates. Worse, at the m=3 threshold the power to detect a true harm of Δlc = −0.20 is P(observed < −0.292 | μ=−0.20, SE≈0.100) ≈ **18%**: a gate that certifies non-harm four times out of five when a −0.2 harm is really present. **Fix, free and offline:** publish the derivation of s_base (what quantity, what df), and resample null10 to report *both* the measured type-I rate **and** a power curve against Δlc ∈ {−0.1, −0.2, −0.3} for m = 1, 3, 5, 10. If power at the m the budget can afford is under 50%, K3′ cannot be described as a non-harm gate — it is a "we could not afford to look" gate, and the seal must say so.

**N2. [FATAL] An undefined, confounded statistic has been promoted to the trigger that concedes the campaign.** §H Options 1 and 4 make `namespace_reuse_rate < 0.15` the kill gate for the entire program. That statistic still has: no denominator (all turns vs `python`-tool turns), no aggregation rule (per-game mean vs pooled over ~1,686 turns), no baseline arm, no null spread, no seed replication (one run, 25 games), and — per §E FATAL #5 — a documented confound in which a low reading is produced by six harness strings telling the model its namespace is ephemeral. A one-seed, one-arm, absolute-cut descriptive is the weakest instrument in the document and it has been given the strongest authority in it. **Fix:** run §5.4's transcript-forensics pass first (free, zero pushes), publish baseline *attempted* cross-turn reference rate with per-game spread, remove the six ephemerality strings from the arm, then set the floor as baseline + δ with δ stated and the estimator frozen. Until then Option 4's off-ramp has no defined trigger.

**N3. [FATAL] §A's entire re-pricing rests on a public–private correlation that has never been estimated, and a free estimate is available.** The claims "public is a noisy proxy (ρ<1)," "E[max@k] on public is largely irrelevant," and "per-draw MEAN is the currency" are all monotone in ρ, which is stated nowhere. If ρ is high, max-selection on public remains near-optimal for the private twin and the daily lottery was not mis-specified; if ρ ≈ 0, then a capability lift's private payoff is also unobservable and Option 1's EV is unestimable, not merely bimodal. **The measurement exists and costs nothing:** public and private are disjoint game sets scored in the same run — exactly the structure of a split-half. Split the 25 public games into halves (repeated random splits), compute run-level score on each half across the 10 null10 runs, take the split-half correlation, apply Spearman–Brown for the 25→50-game set size, and report the interval. That number prices §A, prices Option 3's "harvest the best public draw," and prices whether any free-rail screen is a legitimate proxy (§I Q6). Publish it before R25 closes.

**N4. [MAJOR] The A22 vacatur is a failure-to-reject reported as a pass, on a post-hoc baseline, with a coincidence treated as evidence.** Three defects, all correctable in text: (i) "re-baselined on the 3-run mean: v1 +0.013, v2 −0.107, v2.1 −0.147 — all **PASS** a calibrated line" — with the power computed in N1, PASS here means *uninformative*, not *harmless*; the correct verdict for A22 is "the three screens were uninformative in both directions," which is what "VACATED — UNRESOLVED" should mean.
