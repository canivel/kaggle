## Objections

**[MAJOR] The σ≈0.24 regime is load-bearing and unvalidated on our own process** — Both the "18.5%, i.e. ordinary" exoneration of the 0.65/0.68 pair and the corrected bar 1.1701 are computed under yw8837's externally published duck-family σ≈0.24, while our own 19 API-verified draws give s=0.159 (sealed control 0.134). You cannot borrow another team's dispersion estimate — different hardware, quantization, and eval slice — to set a promotion threshold on our process without at minimum a variance-ratio test showing our sample s is consistent with σ=0.24 (F-test / bootstrap CI on s from n=19; with n=19 and s=0.159, is σ=0.24 even inside the 95% CI? Show it). If σ=0.24 is rejected on our data, the 1.1701 bar is arbitrary; if accepted, the sealed control's s=0.134 was never valid and the whole prereg needs re-derivation, not a patch.

**[MAJOR] The guard options conflate variance inflation with a mean step-down; only option (b)/(c) addresses the actual detected signal** — The change-point scan's finding is a *level* shift (0.973→0.665), not over-dispersion. Raising the bar to 1.1701 (option a) corrects for a wider σ but does nothing if the process mean stepped down: arm-B draws from a degraded regime compared against a stale sealed control mean of 0.9727 produce a biased treatment-effect estimate in *either* direction. The only designs robust to a mean shift are contemporaneous interleaved controls with a paired test (b) or holding for re-check (c). The brief should not present (a) as a standalone admissible option to R23; recommending it as such is a design error.

**[MAJOR] Quoted tail probabilities are not reproducible from the stated parameters — publish the calculation** — Under the sealed control N(0.9727, 0.1343), P(one draw ≤ 0.80) = Φ(−1.286) ≈ 9.9%, so an independent consecutive pair is ≈0.98%, not the quoted 0.38%; and under N(0.9727, 0.24), the pair probability is ≈5.6%, not 18.5% (to get 18.5% per-pair you need μ≈0.84, which is nowhere stated). Either a different event definition (e.g., "≥1 consecutive pair anywhere in the sequence," or the exact values 0.65/0.68 rather than the 0.80 threshold) or a different μ was silently used. Since these two numbers are the quantitative pivot of "NOT tail-consistent" vs "ordinary," attach the script (event definition, μ, σ, independence assumption) to `stationarity_2026-08-02.md` and reconcile before R23 votes.

**[MAJOR] No power / value-of-information analysis for spending 4 gated draws** — The A/B asks to burn 4 of roughly 92 remaining daily draws. With per-draw s in [0.134, 0.24], the standard error of a 4-draw mean is 0.067–0.12, so the 1.1701 bar requires a true effect of roughly +0.20 over control to promote with even coin-flip reliability; the boristown fork evidence is a *single* public draw at 1.47 from a distribution whose own σ you claim may be 0.24 — consistent with a true gate effect anywhere from ~0 to ~+0.5. State the minimum detectable effect at 80% power, the prior over the gate effect implied by the fork-diff analysis, and the expected draws-to-decision under options (a)/(b)/(c). Without this, R23 is ratifying an experiment whose most likely outcome is an uninformative non-promotion.

**[MAJOR] Internal contradiction: "governance-only fire conditions / no evidence blockers" vs. "sealed bar does not survive standalone"** — §1 (entry-gate rail) declares the A/B fire-ready pending only git-seal and ratification; §1 (deep-dive) simultaneously declares the sealed promote bar invalid pending an unresolved stationarity question. **[MINOR] Change-point statistics at n=2 right-segment are near-degenerate** — Welch |t|=8.64 splitting after draw 17 rests on the sd of two points (0.65, 0.68 → sd≈0.021), a known edge pathology of scan statistics; the permutation p=0.0032 partially mitigates the max-over-splits multiplicity but not the tiny-segment variance artifact. The brief acknowledges this qualitatively ("two-point breaks are exactly what these tests over-call") — good — but should add a minimum-segment-length constraint (e.g., ≥3) to the pre-registered scan so this argument isn't re-litigated ad hoc next time.
