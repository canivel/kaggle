## Summary (2 sentences)
The strategic layering (duck floor → search tools → runtime WM → TTT, gated locally) is coherent, but the compute envelope it rests on does not close: the Kaggle GPU quota arithmetic is violated in the two phases that matter most (Phase 0 reproduction and the Phase 4 max-of-N submission plan), and the "primary A/B instrument" (A40 local harness) runs at a per-game wall-clock budget ~20× smaller than the Kaggle operating point on hardware that cannot execute the same FP8 path. Until the wall-clock/quota/parity numbers are re-derived and shown to fit, every local success criterion in the plan is measuring a different system than the one being scored.

## Objections

**[FATAL] The Kaggle GPU quota arithmetic contradicts the plan in Phases 0 and 4.** The budget states 30 h/wk and ~12 h per full submission run. Phase 0 schedules "duck fork ×3" in one week = 36 GPU-h, over quota before smoke tests. Worse, Phase 4 — the phase that actually produces the milestone score — freezes Sep 22 and calls for "6–8 draws" via daily alternating submissions through Sep 29: that is 72–96 GPU-h against ~34 h of quota in that window, so the max-of-N lottery that the entire public-LB-noise mitigation depends on can execute at most 2–3 draws. Either (a) show that scored submission re-runs do not count against the 30 h/wk interactive quota on this competition (cite the comp rules / a test), or (b) rewrite Phase 0 to 2 attempts and Phase 4 to a 2-week staggered freeze (freeze ~Sep 15) so 6+ draws fit. As written, the plan's centerpiece variance-harvesting mechanism is unschedulable.

**[MAJOR] The local A40 harness is not the same operating point as Kaggle — in wall-clock or in hardware — so A/B deltas may not transfer.** "3-seed 25-game sweep ≈ 8 GPU-h" is 75 game-runs → ~6.4 min/game, versus the 2.2 h/game Kaggle budget. Exploration dedup, restart-with-notes voting, and TTT are exactly the components whose payoff is budget-dependent; a +3-level gain at 6 minutes says little about behavior at 2.2 hours (and vice versa — a component that pays only late will look dead locally). Separately, the A40 is Ampere and has **no FP8 tensor-core support**; Qwen 27B "FP8" on A40 runs via weight-only dequant in vLLM with different throughput and numerics than whatever Ada/Hopper-class SKU the Kaggle kernel uses, so tokens-generated-per-game-budget differs between instruments. Fix: measure tokens/s on both platforms, scale the local per-game budget to token-parity (not time-parity), and re-cost the sweep — which will likely be 10–40 GPU-h per sweep, not 8, invalidating the $780 and 40 h/wk figures and the Phase 4 "full ablation matrix" (2³ configs × 3 seeds × 25 games at parity budgets is hundreds of A40-hours).

**[MAJOR] 3 seeds cannot support the pre-registered gates, especially the variance criterion.** A std estimate from n=3 has enormous sampling error (χ² with 2 dof: the sample std routinely deviates ±50% from truth), so "variance reduced ≥30% by voting" is literally undetectable at 3 vs 3 seeds — that gate will resolve by coin flip, reproducing on the local harness the exact disease the proposal diagnoses in the public LB. The level-delta gates ("+≥3 levels aggregate, no game regresses >1 level in ≥2/3 seeds") are also unpowered given duck's own author reports high run-to-run variance. Fix: state the expected per-game seed noise (measure it in Phase 0 with ≥8 seeds once), then derive the minimum seeds per gate; drop the variance-reduction criterion or fund it explicitly (~10 seeds/arm). This interacts with the previous objection: more seeds × parity budgets must still fit 40 h/wk A40, or the schedule slips.

**[MAJOR] "GPU is co-resident and idle between vLLM batches" is not how vLLM works, and the TTT memory budget is unstated.** vLLM pre-allocates ~90% of VRAM for weights + paged KV cache at startup; on a 48 GB card running a ~27 GB-weight model there is no free VRAM pool for a training job unless you lower `gpu_memory_utilization`, which shrinks KV cache, reduces batchable context, and directly costs tokens/s — i.e., wall-clock inside the 2.2 h/game budget. "Idle between batches" also ignores continuous batching and CUDA context/stream contention from a concurrent PyTorch training loop. Fix before Phase 3: a one-day measurement on the A40 of (a) vLLM tokens/s at utilization 0.90 vs 0.75, and (b) CNN train-step latency co-resident vs solo; put the measured throughput tax into the 15%-of-budget kill rule ex ante.

**[MAJOR] The per-run wall-clock accounting inside the kernel is missing.** 25 games × 2.2 h/game = 55 h, but a full run is quoted at 12 h — so games are either parallelized across a multi-GPU SKU or run at far less than 2.2 h each. The proposal never states the Kaggle GPU SKU, vLLM parallelism config, or actual per-game budget realized in the duck notebook, yet Phase 2's voting ("within the 2.2 h/game budget, restart… keep best level") and the global 15%-of-2.2 h kill rule are denominated in a number that may not exist at runtime. Fix: extract the real per-game wall-clock and concurrency from the duck source bundle in Phase 0 and re-denominate all budget-relative criteria.

**[MINOR] Spot A40 + 8-hour sweeps needs preemption handling.** RunPod spot instances get reclaimed; a multi-hour sweep without per-game checkpoint/resume silently corrupts the A/B record. One afternoon of resume logic in eval_harness, and log the instance type per sweep so preempted runs are excluded from paired comparisons.

**[MINOR] BFS/planning inside the 60 s tool-loop.** The team's own memory (`feedback_arc_long_bfs_mcts`) says uncapped search blows budgets; `bfs_solve()` and BFS-through-model must ship with hard wall-clock caps and anytime behavior (return best partial plan) from day one, not after the first timeout post-mortem. The proposal gestures at caps in Risk 5 — make them numeric in Phase 1's spec.
