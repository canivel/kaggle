## Summary (2 sentences)
The brief demonstrates admirable pre-registration discipline (two lines closed by their own gates, no discretion exercised), but the surviving plan is a ranked backlog of cheap prompt/harness hygiene with no per-candidate falsifiable effect-size targets, while the only mechanism nominated to close the 0.98→1.44 gap ("grinder cracking," build NLT Jul 20) has no design, no metric, and no gate anywhere in the document. From a synthesis/verification standpoint, the campaign has also just used an offline build rail to falsify a mechanism (banking/soft-time) whose trigger condition arguably cannot occur in the offline regime — a regime-validity error that the authors themselves flag as a "tension" and then wave through.

## Objections

**[FATAL] The sole remaining wall-closer is unspecified and ungated** — §Instruments declares "R3–R5 grinder cracking now the ONLY live budgeted wall-closer" with build work due in 2 days (NLT Jul 20), yet the document contains zero design, zero pre-registered success criterion, and zero expected-Δlc for it; the panel is instead asked to spend its full attention ranking seven marginal-efficiency candidates (Q1) whose combined plausible effect is nowhere argued to exceed the ~0.14 MDE, let alone the ~+0.46 needed to reach the 1.44 wall. A plan whose only load-bearing component is absent from the plan is not reviewable as a strategy. Fix: before any build work, publish the grinder-cracking spec with (i) the mechanism class (search? synthesized game-specific policy? model swap?), (ii) a pre-registered offline gate with prongs and α, (iii) an expected per-draw Δ with its basis. If that cannot be produced by Jul 20, the deadline in A6 — not the panel — is the thing that should slip.

**[MAJOR] Build-rail falsification of banking is regime-invalid; the gate refuted a composition, not components** — The A1 gate tested warpack-v1 = banking+recovery+retry_guard *composed*, on an offline bench the authors admit "forces" a regime where "soft-time banking/recovery conditions differ" from the 8h LB budget. A mechanism whose firing condition (budget pressure) is suppressed by the test harness cannot be falsified by that harness — the experiment has no power against the hypothesis it is used to kill, exactly analogous to the ledger's 0-escalation finding. Yet Q1(e) is "parked with its line" on this evidence, while the descriptive LB signal (3/4 draws ≥ 0.91, Δ+0.058) points the other way. Fix: either (a) show the build rail's action-budget profile actually triggers banking's soft-time path (log the trigger counter, as was done for the ledger canary — if triggers > 0 and Δ ≈ 0, the parking is sound), or (b) reclassify (e) from "refuted-adjacent" to "untested in the relevant regime" and let it compete in Q1 on that honest grade.

**[MAJOR] Q1 candidates carry process grades, not falsifiable predictions** — Every candidate is graded "distill-grounded / cheap / medium complexity" — properties of provenance and cost, not of expected effect. Nothing in Q1 states, for any candidate, a predicted Δlc or ΔRHAE, the trigger-frequency estimate that would bound its maximum possible effect (e.g., how many scored actions per draw are currently lost to unseen budget deaths, for (a); how many verbatim re-submits occur per draw, for (c)), or the pre-registered pass rule for the war-v3.0 window. The gate machinery is excellent but it is pointed at hypotheses with no quantitative content, so "pass" and "fail" are both uninformative about the gap. Fix: for each Q1 candidate, extract from the existing distill traces a *counting* upper bound (events-per-draw × max-value-per-event) and rank on that; candidates whose upper bound is < MDE/2 should not consume a window alone.

**[MAJOR] The one genuine world-model candidate, Q1(d), is deferred without a cheap offline metric that would cost nothing to run** — PREDICT→RESULT wiring is the only backlog item that builds a checkable predictive model of game dynamics, and it is precisely what gives the ledger a firing trigger (the diagnosed failure of war-v2). Relegating it to "A/B-only" while promoting (f), a prompt fix, ignores that (d) admits a *free, non-LB* synthesis-quality metric: prediction accuracy (fraction of PREDICT lines contradicted by RESULT, per game, on replayed deterministic traces per N5). If predictions are near-chance, (d) is dead cheaply; if they separate ka59-like games from cn04-like games, you have the trigger signal that (g)'s allocation policy also needs. Fix: run the prediction-accuracy measurement offline on existing N5 replay data before ranking (d), and report it with a pre-registered threshold.

**[MAJOR] "su15 = info-theoretic wall (accept)" is an impossibility claim with no argument** — An information-theoretic wall is a theorem-shaped statement: it requires showing the observation channel provably does not expose the latent state needed to act, not that one probe tier failed. The document offers only a GPT-5.6 probe decomposition as authority, and Q4 proposes to spend nothing further on the basis of this unproven claim. Fix: either publish the argument (what information is missing, why no policy in the harness's action space can extract it — the su15 game-over-continuation bug in (f) suggests the prior probes may have been confounded by the very deadlock being fixed), or downgrade to "empirically unresponsive at probed tiers" and re-evaluate after (f) ships, since (f) directly changes su15's reachable state space.

**[MINOR] Ledger refutation magnitude rests on one seed** — The mechanistic core of the war-v2-eval verdict (1552 digests, 0 escalations → no path to benefit) is sound and I accept the qualitative refutation, but the quantitative "constant context tax" of Δlc −0.128 is a single-seed estimate (`war_v2_eval_s1`) with p 0.86, and it is being cited as if it bounds the cost of any future ledger. Fix: label the −0.128 as unestimated noise; the reusable finding is trigger-rate = 0, nothing more.

**[MINOR] Q1(g) violates the stated "all game-agnostic" constraint as instantiated, and its classification data is fragile** — "Starve stable negatives like cn04" hard-codes decisions from 3 build-rail seeds whose per-game deltas the gate itself just showed to be noisy (LOO p range [0.14, 0.42]). If (g) is scoped, the fire/starve classifier must be an online, pre-registered rule computed from within-draw evidence (e.g., level-clear rate by budget fraction), not a frozen list from the bench.
